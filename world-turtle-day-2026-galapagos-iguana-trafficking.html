200 Million Years Ago
Turtles Appear on Earth
Turtles predate the dinosaurs by tens of millions of years. Their shell — a body plan unchanged for millennia — helped them survive every mass extinction on this planet. Until now.
1975
CITES Comes Into Force — Galápagos Iguanas First Protected
The Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species enters into force. Galápagos iguanas receive early listing. Decades later, all four Galápagos iguana species are elevated to CITES Appendix I — the highest available protection, effectively banning commercial international trade.
Year 2000
World Turtle Day Founded
American Tortoise Rescue establishes World Turtle Day, observed every May 23. Now in its 26th year, the observance mobilises global awareness around escalating threats to turtles, tortoises, and their habitats.
2025 — Research Published
CITES Permit Laundering of Galápagos Iguanas Exposed
A peer-reviewed study in Biological Conservation reveals how traffickers falsely claim wild-caught Galápagos iguanas are “captive-bred” to obtain valid CITES export permits. Uganda is identified as a key transit hub. Once laundered, animals move internationally with paperwork nearly indistinguishable from legitimately bred stock.
February 20, 2026
158 Floreana Giant Tortoises Return — After 180 Years
For the first time since the 1800s, 158 juvenile tortoises carrying Floreana lineage DNA are released onto their ancestral island. Driven extinct by whalers and invasive animals, the lineage was reconstructed from genetic descendants found on Isabela Island. Each juvenile carries 40–80% of the original species’ genetic makeup.
May 19, 2026
Guayaquil Airport Arrest — 12 Marine Iguanas Seized
Ecuadorian National Police arrest three Thai nationals at José Joaquín de Olmedo International Airport. Their handbags held 12 Galápagos marine iguanas with legs tightly bound. One was already dead. A court orders pretrial detention. Ecuador’s wildlife trafficking law carries up to three years in prison.
Same Week, May 2026
Four More Iguanas Found Discarded on Guayaquil Streets
Ecuador’s Ministry of Environment and Energy reports four separate cases of marine iguanas found on Guayaquil sidewalks in the same week as the airport arrest — bringing the total suspected smuggled animals to 16 in roughly seven days.
May 23, 2026
World Turtle Day — 26th Annual Observance
The 26th annual World Turtle Day is observed globally. American Tortoise Rescue co-founder Susan Tellem states: “Turtles survived the dinosaurs. It would be a tragedy if they couldn’t survive us.” The green sea turtle’s reclassification from Endangered to Least Concern is noted alongside the ongoing crisis facing 61% of all turtle species.

What You Can Do — Practical Steps
01
Never buy live reptiles or turtle products without verified legal provenance. “Captive-bred” labels can be falsified — CITES permit laundering is a documented, active mechanism used by traffickers.
02
Report suspected wildlife trafficking anonymously through the UNODC World Wildlife Crime Reporting system at unodc.org — tips from the public have led to documented arrests.
03
Reduce single-use plastic. Sea turtles and marine iguanas mistake floating plastic debris for food. Ocean plastic kills long before traffickers reach an animal.
04
Support verified conservation programmes: the Galápagos Conservancy and Island Conservation run the tortoise breeding and reintroduction projects behind the Floreana comeback of 2026.
