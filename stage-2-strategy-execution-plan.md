## CONTENT STANDARDS (All Articles)

### Factual Requirements
- Cross-verified facts from Stage 1 primary sources only
- Specific data, statistics, technical details, key numbers included
- Direct quotes from sources/experts where available
- Journalistic neutrality - no promotional language
- No speculation without evidence
- Distinguish predictions from documented facts

### Style Requirements
- Simple, accessible language for all readers
- Shorter, clearer sentences
- Practical benefits for families explained
- Avoids complex terminology where possible
- Connects to daily life where relevant
- Humanlike, not robotic tone
- Professional but approachable voice

### SEO/AEO Optimization
- Strategic keyword placement (from Stage 1 keyword clusters)
- Varied sentence lengths
- Clear hierarchy with minimal subheadings
- Accessible language for featured snippets
- Question-based search intent addressed

### Restrictions
- NO flowery language ("tapestry," "beacon," "symphony")
- NO excessive headings/subheadings/lists
- NO attribution phrases ("according to," "source document")
- NO copying sentences directly from source material
- NO source document wording
- NO repetition of past routines
- NO over-explanation of obvious concepts

### Length Guidelines
- **News updates/announcements:** 300-400 words (facts only)
- **Explainers/research/economic stories:** 500-700 words (terms explained)
- **Human-interest/obituary:** 400-600 words (context + impact)

---

## THREE-STEP VERIFICATION PROCESS (Per Article)

### Step 1: Initial Draft
- Write article following all content standards
- Include all Stage 1 research insights
- Apply logical/common-sense factors where reasonable
- Optimize for SEO/AEO
- Target specified word length

### Step 2: Factuality Check
- Verify every fact against Stage 1 primary sources
- Identify any non-factual information
- Flag logical additions vs. source-based facts
- List all numbers, dates, claims to be verified
- Check: Are all statements supported by provided sources?

### Step 3: Final Factual Version
- Fix identified inaccuracies without rewriting
- Preserve language, sentence structure, word choices
- Keep logical/common-sense factors intact
- Provide final version ready for publication
- Confirm: All facts verified from Stage 1 sources
