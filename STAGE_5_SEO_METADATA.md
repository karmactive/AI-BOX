The breach occurred between March 10 and March 16 this year when someone accessed CareCloud's Amazon Web Services database without permission. The company initially reported 345,000 affected individuals when filing with the [Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar). The number of potentially affected individuals later reported to the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services was 3,756,469.

However, the full scale of the data exposure remained unclear for months. Breach notices indicate exposure of personal, financial and medical information including names, dates of birth, Social Security numbers, and insurance information. Medical data attracts criminal attention because it combines personal identity information with payment details and healthcare history, making it valuable for fraudulent applications and identity theft.

CareCloud responded by isolating the affected database environment and brought in third-party specialists to investigate how the breach happened. The company's remediation included identity protection and credit monitoring services for affected individuals.

[Healthcare organizations handle sensitive information](/health/) about millions of Americans daily. Data security in the medical field matters because compromised records can affect people's insurance coverage, credit scores, and ability to access legitimate medical care.

## STORY 2: SHINGLES VACCINE LINKED TO LOWER CARDIOVASCULAR RISK IN OLDER ADULTS

### STAGE 4 ARTICLE (WITH LINKS)
New research suggests that receiving a recombinant shingles vaccine may reduce cardiovascular disease risk in older adults. A study published in [Nature Medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04606-0) tracked 72,920 matched individuals and found those receiving the recombinant vaccine experienced significantly lower rates of heart disease and stroke over seven years.

The study compared two matched cohorts of 36,460 adults each. The 2018 cohort had 93.5 percent exposure to Shingrix, the recombinant zoster vaccine. The 2017 cohort had 98.6 percent exposure to Zostavax, the live-attenuated vaccine. Both vaccines prevent shingles, but their effects on cardiovascular outcomes differed substantially in this population.

Over seven years, people who received Shingrix showed a 9 percent lower cardiovascular disease burden compared to those who received Zostavax. The cardiovascular benefits included a 10 percent reduction in ischemic heart disease, a 12 percent reduction in heart failure, a 12 percent reduction in ischemic stroke among men, and a 7 percent reduction in atrial fibrillation.

Scientists propose that the recombinant vaccine's specific composition triggers different immune responses than the older live-virus version. However, researchers explicitly noted that causal inference requires additional assumptions and that unmeasured factors cannot be excluded as contributing to the differences observed. The study authors explicitly called for clinical trials and mechanistic studies to better understand the mechanisms behind the observed cardiovascular differences.

In the United States, Shingrix is currently the available shingles vaccine for older adults. Zostavax was discontinued by its manufacturer Merck and officially withdrawn from clinical use on November 18, 2020, making the study's comparison between the two vaccines an analysis of the historical transition period between vaccine types.

This finding matters because cardiovascular disease remains a leading cause of death and disability. [Adults over 50 who receive shingles vaccination](/aging/) should understand that the cardiovascular benefits documented in this study occurred over a multi-year period in a large matched population, not necessarily in every individual who receives the vaccine.

Static fire tests involve igniting rocket engines while the vehicle remains secured to the ground. For Starship's Super Heavy booster, coordinating 33 engines simultaneously requires extremely precise timing and control.

A successful static fire demonstrates successful test operation under controlled conditions. The test verified propellant flow and engine controller function while the vehicle remained secured. [Static fire completion](/space/) provides engineers data for understanding engine performance and system responses, contributing to the overall development sequence.

Starship represents SpaceX's next-generation heavy-lift launch system designed to carry large payloads to orbit. The Super Heavy booster provides initial thrust to lift the entire system. Ship 41, the upper stage vehicle, is designed to continue the mission following separation from the booster.

Flight 14 will be an orbital test flight, meaning Starship will attempt to reach orbit around Earth. This marks progression from earlier test flights that focused on controlled ground operations and vehicle recovery procedures.

The Federal Aviation Administration oversees commercial spaceflight licensing and safety. SpaceX operates under [FAA commercial space transportation authority](https://www.spacex.com/), which requires companies to demonstrate operational capability through sequential testing and flight validation. ## STORY 5: TROPICAL STORM DOLLY WEAKENS, NHC ISSUES FINAL ADVISORY

### STAGE 4 ARTICLE (WITH LINKS)
Tropical Storm Dolly has weakened into a remnant tropical wave as it encountered unfavorable wind patterns. The National Hurricane Center issued its final advisory on August 28, indicating transition from tropical storm classification to remnant tropical wave status. The system's final forecast projected dissipation by August 30, with Advisory No. 5 designated as the last forecast advisory issued on the system.

The tropical wave system, designated AL042026, formed in the central Atlantic before encountering strong vertical wind shear. Shear occurs when winds at different altitudes blow in different directions or speeds, disrupting the organized circulation that defines a tropical storm. Wind shear of 25 to 35 knots pulled the storm structure apart by separating upper clouds from the warm core beneath.

As Dolly moved westward toward the Caribbean basin, wind shear conditions intensified. The [National Hurricane Center's advisory](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/) indicated the system had transitioned from a closed circulation to an open tropical wave, meaning the spinning center that characterized the tropical storm no longer existed as a distinct feature.

Remnant tropical waves can occasionally regenerate into storms if they reach sufficiently warm water and encounter calmer wind patterns. However, current atmospheric analysis indicated that regeneration remained unlikely for Dolly. The system was expected to continue as a dispersed wave pattern rather than reorganize into a concentrated low-pressure system.

[The Atlantic hurricane season runs from June through November](/environment/), with peak tropical activity occurring during late summer and fall months. During this period, warm ocean water and atmospheric conditions can support tropical system development, though not every tropical wave becomes operationally significant.

Residents in Atlantic and Caribbean regions monitor tropical activity through National Hurricane Center advisories. The final forecast status on August 28 indicated minimal threat from the system as it transitioned away from organized tropical circulation.
