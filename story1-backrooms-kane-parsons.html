The Road to No. 1 — Tap Each Stage to Expand
2019
The Internet Myth That Started It All
A single photograph of a yellow-carpeted, fluorescent-lit empty room circulates on 4chan with a caption describing it as a place people "no-clip" into by accident. It becomes one of the internet's most enduring urban legends — the Backrooms creepypasta. The image works because it induces liminal horror: spaces that look familiar but feel inherently wrong.
January 7, 2022
Kane Pixels Posts the Video That Changes Everything
Still in high school, Kane Parsons posts a 10-minute analog horror short on his YouTube channel using Blender — free, open-source 3D software. Disguised with a VHS filter to make the CGI pass as found footage, the clip shows a camera operator lost in the Backrooms. It accumulates over 79 million views by May 2026, becoming the benchmark for Backrooms content worldwide. Studios notice.
Early 2023
A24 Makes the Call. Parsons Skips College.
While applying to universities, Parsons begins fielding adaptation offers from major studios. A24 makes its bid — and he accepts. He defers university to pursue the film. Horror director Osgood Perkins (Longlegs, The Monkey) joins as mentor and co-producer alongside James Wan and Shawn Levy. It is announced that Parsons, who was still in high school, will direct.
Summer–August 2025
30,000 Square Feet of Sets. Four Sound Stages.
Filming begins with production designer Danny Vermette adapting Parsons' original Blender digital designs into physical construction — over 30,000 square feet of Backrooms sets across four sound stages. The $10 million production casts Chiwetel Ejiofor, Renate Reinsve, Mark Duplass, Avan Jogia (a fan of the original series), Finn Bennett, and Lukita Maxwell.
May 29, 2026
Opening Weekend. Every Record Falls.
Backrooms opens to $81.5M domestically and $118M worldwide — A24's biggest debut ever, more than tripling the studio's prior record. Kane Parsons becomes the youngest filmmaker to top the domestic box office, breaking the record Josh Trank held since 2012's Chronicle (when Trank was 27). It is also the largest opening for original horror and the best start for a debut non-franchise director in history.
June 2026
$100M Domestic. Sequel Confirmed. What's Next.
Backrooms crosses $100 million domestically in six days — A24's first. Total worldwide gross exceeds $141 million. Parsons confirms a sequel is in development and is seeking a screenwriting collaborator to expand the Backrooms universe into a feature anthology. He is also developing a separate original film with Osgood Perkins, expected through A24 or Neon. The same weekend, fellow YouTuber-director Curry Barker's Obsession also crossed $100M, while Disney's Mandalorian & Grogu stalled. A generational shift in who makes what Hollywood watches.
