**Correction note:** Add a line stating that the fees had already been actively applied to some vessels from March 2026, with the parliamentary legislation formalizing what was already operational practice.

## STORY 4: TROPICAL DEPRESSION MOKE — HAWAII

### ✅ CONFIRMED FACTS

| Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|

| Maximum sustained winds of 35 mph | ✅ Confirmed | NOAA NHC advisory |

| Gusty winds possible | ✅ Confirmed | NOAA NHC |
| Hawai'i Emergency Management Agency official page active | ✅ Confirmed | dod.hawaii.gov/hiema |

### ❌ MAJOR FACTUAL ERROR FOUND — SAFETY CRITICAL

**Sentence in article:**
> "localized rainfall accumulating **one to two inches** could occur across higher terrain"

**What is wrong — this is the most serious error in all five articles.**
NOAA advisories and multiple reputable weather sources state a dramatically higher rainfall forecast:

- **As Tropical Depression:** NOAA forecast **3 to 6 inches** across the Big Island, with life-threatening flooding and mudslide potential (confirmed by NOAA NHC)
- **As Tropical Storm (prior stage):** Forecast of **5 to 10 inches** with **maximum amounts up to 15 inches** (confirmed by CBS News, Weather.com)
- NPR reported "heavy rainfall" with life-threatening flooding
- ABC News reported "heavy rain to the Big Island"

The article's "1 to 2 inches" figure is **3 to 8 times less** than what NOAA reported. This error significantly understates the hazard to Hawaii residents and runs counter to the article's own safety-focused intent. This is a critical correction.

**Correction:**
> "NOAA forecast rainfall totals of **3 to 6 inches** across the Big Island, with maximum amounts potentially reaching **15 inches**, which could produce **life-threatening flooding and mudslides**, particularly in steep terrain and areas already saturated from Hurricane Lala."

**Sources:** [NOAA NHC Advisory](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/mobile/text/refresh/HFOTCPCP3+html/230237.shtml) | [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/tropical-storm-moke-rain-hawaii-big-island/) | [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/23/nx-s1-5941769/tropical-storm-moke-hawaii) | [Hawaii News Now](https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com/2026/08/24/moke-weakens-tropical-depression-rain-gusty-winds-still-possible-hawaii-island/)
