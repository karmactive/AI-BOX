## PART 1: INTERNAL LINKING STRATEGY

### What is Internal Linking?
Hyperlinks that point from one page on Karmactive.COM to another page on Karmactive.COM. 
These links improve SEO and increase page views by keeping readers on the site.

### Internal Linking Benefits:
- ✅ Increases time on site and page views
- ✅ Distributes page authority across site
- ✅ Helps search engines understand site structure
- ✅ Improves keyword rankings for linked pages
- ✅ Improves user navigation and experience

## BACKLINK OUTREACH PROCESS

### Step 1: Identify Contact Editors
- Find section editors: "sports@", "politics@", "military@"
- Use LinkedIn to find journalists covering these beats
- Visit outlet's "Contact Us" page for correct email

### Step 2: Craft Outreach Email

**Template:**
```
Subject: Breaking News Feature: [Article Title] - May Interest [Outlet Name] Readers

Dear [Editor/Writer Name],

We've published an in-depth analysis on Karmactive.COM covering 
[story angle]. This may be of interest to your [section] readers.

Article: [Article Headline]
URL: [Full URL]
Key Findings: [1-2 bullet points]

Would this be of interest for coverage or mention in your [section]?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
Karmactive.COM
```

### Step 3: Monitor Backlinks
- Use Google Search Console to track referring domains
- Use Ahrefs or SEMrush to monitor backlink growth
- Track Karmactive.COM's Domain Authority (DA) improvement

## LINK BUILDING BEST PRACTICES

### ✅ DO:
- Create genuinely valuable, fact-checked content (✅ You have this)
- Include high-authority external links (✅ You have this)
- Use descriptive anchor text
- Build relationships with journalists and editors
- Share content on social media (can attract organic links)
- Write for industry publications (guest posts link back)
- Participate in expert roundups and interviews

### ❌ DON'T:
- Buy backlinks (Google penalty risk)
- Create private blog networks (PBNs) — Google penalty
- Use keyword-stuffed anchor text ("cheap widgets")
- Link to low-quality or spammy sites
- Use automated link-building tools
- Participate in link schemes or exchanges
- Hide links with white text or tiny fonts
