Celebrity Relationships

Madison Beer is engaged. The 27-year-old singer said yes to Los Angeles Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert on July 28, 2026, after a proposal staged beneath an arbor of white flowers. Beer shared the news on her official Instagram with two words: "Meet my fiancé."

Herbert, 28, posted the same photos on his own account. One image shows him on one knee, ring box open, while Beer crouches in front of him mid-reaction. Within a day, the joint post had drawn more than 5.7 million likes, with the NFL's own account among those leaving congratulations below it.

Beer and Herbert were first photographed together in August 2025, while she was filming one of her music videos around Los Angeles. Neither confirmed anything at the time. That changed on October 5, 2025, when Beer was seen kissing Herbert on the sideline before the Chargers' game against the Washington Commanders, a moment that settled months of speculation without either of them saying a word to a reporter.

Jewelry experts who reviewed the engagement photos estimated the center stone at more than 15 carats, cut in an elongated cushion shape, with a value they placed anywhere from $500,000 to over $1 million. Neither Beer nor Herbert has confirmed a price. The ring shows up clearly across every angle Beer posted, which is likely why it became the second most discussed detail of the announcement, right after the caption itself.

August 2025Rumors start on a video set

Beer and Herbert were photographed together while she was shooting a music video around Los Angeles. Photos of the two later leaving a friend's house in Redondo Beach followed, and dating speculation began.

October 5, 2025A sideline kiss confirms it

Beer was seen kissing Herbert on the sideline before the Chargers hosted the Washington Commanders. It was the clearest public confirmation of the relationship to that point.

March 5, 2026Herbert's birthday post for Beer

On Beer's birthday, Herbert posted a black-and-white photo of the two of them with a caption that read:

"Happy birthday to my favorite person of all time. I love you so much. You've changed my life forever."
March 10, 2026Beer returns the favor

Five days later, on Herbert's birthday, Beer posted Instagram stories that read:

"happy birthday baby boy, i am so lucky to be yours, you are my dream come true... life saver... love u so"
July 13, 2026Beer wraps her Locket Tour

Beer closed out The Locket Tour, her fourth headlining run, with a show in New York City, ending a stretch of 32 dates across Europe and North America that started in May.

July 28, 2026The proposal and the announcement

Herbert proposed beneath a flower-covered arbor. Beer posted the photos with the caption "Meet my fiancé," and Herbert posted the same set on his own account soon after.
