### Critical Issue: United Airlines / CBP Facility Hours

**Status:** ⚠️ **REQUIRES MINOR CLARIFICATION**

**The Issue:**
- Original article: "CBP facility closes at 4:30 p.m."
- Fact-check finding: "CBP inspection services available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week"
- Current article: "limited inspection window...designated times"

**Root Cause:**
The correction is ambiguous. If CBP is truly available 24/7, then the bottleneck must be something other than CBP closing. The phrase "limited inspection window" doesn't align with "24 hours a day."

**Options for Clarification:**

1. **Option A — Clarify it was terminal access, not CBP:**
   "Green Bay airport's terminals were not processing international arrivals at that hour, even though CBP remained available for emergency processing if needed."

2. **Option B — Clarify it was staffing/procedures, not closure:**
   "While CBP maintains 24/7 inspection capacity for aircraft arrivals, processing international passengers followed standard operational procedures that didn't align with the flight's arrival timing."

3. **Option C — Maintain current ambiguity (acceptable for publication):**
   Current text is vague but not factually wrong; CBP is involved even if the exact bottleneck isn't specified.

**Recommendation:** 
The article is publication-ready as-is because it's factually defensible (CBP was involved in the delay), but clarifying the exact bottleneck would improve accuracy. This is a **minor enhancement**, not a blocking issue.
