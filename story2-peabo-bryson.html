In Memoriam
Peabo Bryson
April 13, 1951 — June 2, 2026

Two Grammy Awards. Two Disney classics. The first animated song in history to reach No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100. A voice that sang at the edge of Whitney Houston's record — and made a generation feel it.

A Five-Decade Career — Tap Each Stage to Expand
~1965
First Stage — Backup at 14
Robert Peapo Bryson begins singing professionally at 14 as a backup vocalist for Al Freeman and the Upsetters. Freeman's difficulty pronouncing his French West-Indian given name, "Peapo," produces the stage name "Peabo." The slight mispronunciation becomes part of music history.
1976
Debut Album Alongside Luther Vandross and Cissy Houston
Bryson releases his self-titled debut "Peabo," recorded with Luther Vandross and Cissy Houston. The album establishes his warm tenor as one of R&B's most distinctive voices and begins a decades-long career on Capitol Records.
1978
Two Gold Albums — Reaching for the Sky & Crosswinds
Capitol Records releases "Reaching for the Sky" and "Crosswinds" in the same year — both albums are certified Gold. Bryson spends the following decade building a deep R&B catalog that earns him respect among both fans and fellow artists.
1991
"Beauty and the Beast" with Celine Dion
Disney's animated film "Beauty and the Beast" features Bryson and Celine Dion on its title track. The collaboration wins the Grammy for Best Pop Performance by a Duo or Group in 1993. Dion would later describe Bryson as making her "so comfortable" as she was learning to sing in English — testament to the warmth he brought to every collaboration.
1992 / 1993
"A Whole New World" — The First Disney #1
Recorded in 1992 for Disney's "Aladdin" with Regina Belle, "A Whole New World" reaches No. 1 on the US Billboard Hot 100 on the chart dated March 6, 1993 — replacing Whitney Houston's "I Will Always Love You," which had held the top position for a record 14 consecutive weeks. It is the first song from any Disney animated film to top the chart. The next would not come for 29 years.
2010
Marriage to Tanya Boniface
Bryson marries Tanya Boniface, a singer and former member of the English R&B group The 411. In 2018, they welcome a son. Bryson also has a daughter from a previous relationship and three grandchildren. He continues performing and recording into his seventies.
June 2, 2026
Peabo Bryson Passes at 75
Bryson dies at 5:00 p.m. ET at his home in Marietta, Georgia, from complications of a stroke suffered on May 31. He is surrounded by family and those closest to him. Tributes pour in from Celine Dion, Regina Belle, and music fans worldwide. His music continues to stream across every platform on earth.

From Those Who Shared the Stage
Celine Dion — via Instagram
"He was so wonderful and generous to me all those years ago when we recorded 'Beauty and the Beast.' He made me so comfortable, as I was just learning to sing in English. He will remain for me always as a real symbol of the joy that music has brought to my life. His voice and his talent will be missed."
Regina Belle — Official Statement
"Peabo was like a brother to me. I held his hand and sang in a whisper, 'A Whole New World' and 'Total Praise' — our version of the song that Peabo and I recorded."

"For more than five decades, Peabo's extraordinary voice served as the soundtrack to some of life's most cherished moments. His music carried generations through joyful celebrations, great love stories and enduring moments of comfort and inspiration, creating a legacy that will forever live in the hearts of those who loved him."

— Official Statement, The Bryson Family, June 2026
