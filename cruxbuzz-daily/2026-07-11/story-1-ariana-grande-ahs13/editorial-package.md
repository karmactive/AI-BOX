- Grande will not appear in AHS Season 13; reported July 10, 2026, first by Deadline, citing sources. FX and 20th Television declined to comment. No first-hand statement from Grande, Ryan Murphy, or FX exists as of July 11.
- Reason (per sourced trade reporting): the production schedule was originally built so she could film while touring; shooting dates moved to summer 2026 and collided with locked tour dates. She filmed no scenes. Her role was never publicly revealed.
- Casting was announced October 31, 2025 in a Halloween cast video ending with the on-screen text "Surprise, bitch. I bet you thought you'd seen the last of me."
- Announced cast: Sarah Paulson, Evan Peters, Angela Bassett, Kathy Bates, Emma Roberts, Billie Lourd, Gabourey Sidibe, Leslie Grossman, Jessica Lange (plus Grande; more names added through 2026). Set photos published in spring 2026 showed the Robichaux Academy set rebuilt (Coven connection), with filming in New York.
- Premiere: Thursday, September 24, 2026, 9 p.m. ET on FX and Hulu (Disney+ internationally), announced July 9, 2026 alongside a San Diego Comic-Con "13 Flavors" activation. NOTE: most coverage words this as a same-date FX/Hulu premiere; do not write "next-day Hulu."
- The Eternal Sunshine Tour: her fifth tour; opened June 6, 2026 at Oakland Arena; closes September 1, 2026 at The O2, London; 41 shows in 10 US/Canada/UK cities; ~6 million users in the presale queue; London sold out in about 15 minutes; in late June she moved select July dates (Brooklyn July 12 → 14; Boston July 22/24 → 23/26).
- History: Scream Queens (2015) with Ryan Murphy, Emma Roberts and Billie Lourd; first Oscar nomination (Best Supporting Actress) for Wicked; Wicked: For Good released November 21, 2025. Jessica Lange returns after two AHS Emmy wins (2012, 2014). Franchise: 90+ Emmy nominations, 700M+ hours streamed globally (FX press figure).
