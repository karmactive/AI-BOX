Ariana Grande is out of American Horror Story Season 13. Reports on July 10, 2026, citing production sources, said a change in the show's shooting schedule collided with the locked dates of her Eternal Sunshine Tour, and that she filmed no scenes for the season. FX and 20th Television have made no public comment, and Grande has not addressed the exit on her official Instagram. The timing stings for fans: FX had confirmed the season's September 24 premiere date only one day earlier. Casting shake-ups have been a steady theme in screen news this year, from A24's star-led production in Boston to new-season builds across prestige TV like Vince Gilligan's Pluribus.

The scale gap explains the outcome. The Eternal Sunshine Tour, her fifth, opened June 6 at Oakland Arena and runs through September 1 at The O2 in London, with roughly six million users in the presale queue and London selling out in about 15 minutes, per figures around the on-sale. Full dates sit on arianagrande.com and Ticketmaster. An arena tour of that size cannot move for a supporting TV role — and in late June, Grande had already shuffled July dates in Brooklyn and Boston, an early sign the calendar was under strain.

Reports citing production sources say the shooting schedule — originally built so Grande could film while touring — moved into summer dates that conflicted with her locked tour routing. She shot nothing for the season. FX and 20th Television decline to comment.
