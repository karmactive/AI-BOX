Box Office · Disney · Opening Weekend

Disney's live-action Moana sailed into 3,875 North American theaters on Friday, July 10, 2026 — and its maiden voyage is rougher than the studio hoped. After $4.5 million in Thursday previews and a Friday of roughly $17 million, trade projections now put the three-day opening at $40 million to $45 million, well under the $60 million-plus that pre-release tracking suggested. The film, directed by Hamilton's Thomas Kail with Catherine Laga'aia in her screen debut and Dwayne Johnson back as Maui, carries a reported $250 million budget before marketing. Disney's wider theatrical chessboard is already in motion this year, from the Avengers: Doomsday move to December to the Pirates of the Caribbean reboot.

All figures below are trade-reported estimates as of Saturday, July 11, 2026. Studios issue official weekend estimates on Sunday.

Where Moana Lands Among Disney's Remakes — Tap Any Bar

Disney live-action remake openings (domestic, 3-day)

Studio-reported opening weekends. Moana shown at its current projection midpoint; tap a bar for context.

The comparison every number-watcher is making sits at the bottom of that chart: Snow White opened to $42.2 million in 2025, and Moana's projection lands in the same band — with a budget more than twice as large. The counter-example is only a year old, too. Lilo & Stitch opened to $146 million in May 2025 on a reported $100 million budget and swam past $1 billion worldwide. Timing may be part of the answer: Moana 2 was still setting Thanksgiving records in November 2024, roughly 20 months before this remake reached theaters, and the two animated films together grossed roughly $1.7 billion worldwide — Disney detailed Moana 2's record run on its own newsroom. Reviews add headwind: Rotten Tomatoes sits in the mid-30s and Metacritic at 41 as of Saturday, with Laga'aia's lead performance the one element critics consistently praise. Audience scores and CinemaScore, which often tell a friendlier story for family titles, arrive with Sunday's estimates on Rotten Tomatoes and Box Office Mojo.

Warner Bros. and New Line's Evil Dead Burn, directed by Infested's Sébastien Vaniček and led by Souheila Yacoub, is projected between $25 million and $35 million. Anything mid-range or better beats the franchise's best openings — $25.8 million for the 2013 reboot and $24.5 million for Evil Dead Rise in 2023, a film that turned roughly $19 million of budget into $147 million worldwide. On a weekend when the giant wobbles, the sixth Evil Dead entry may deliver the healthiest return in theaters — the same low-cost horror economics we tracked around the Companion trailer launch.

The People Behind the Voyage, In Their Own Words

"Humbled to say we're bringing Moana's beautiful story to the live-action big screen! Maui changed my life (miss you grandpa) and I'm honored to partner with Disney to tell our story through the realm of music and dance, which at the core is who we are as Polynesian people."

— Dwayne Johnson, announcing the film on his X account, April 3, 2023

"My grandfather comes from Fa'aala, Palauli, in Savai'i. And my grandmother is from Leulumoega Tuai on the main island of 'Upolu in Samoa." She added she was honored "to represent young girls who look like me."

— Catherine Laga'aia, Disney casting announcement, June 2024

"I'm truly honored to pass this baton to the next young woman of Pacific Island descent, to honor our incredible Pacific peoples, cultures and communities."

— Auli'i Cravalho, the original Moana and this film's executive producer, in her own social video, May 2024

Laga'aia, a Sydney-born performer of Samoan heritage and daughter of actor Jay Laga'aia, fronts a cast that includes John Tui as Chief Tui, Frankie Adams as Sina and Rena Owen as Gramma Tala, with Lin-Manuel Miranda returning to write a new song, "Along the Way," performed by Laga'aia, Johnson and Cravalho. Disney again worked with the Oceanic Cultural Trust — the Pacific advisory group it describes on its corporate site — spanning tattoo, choreography, history and language specialists. Filming ran from July to November 2024 in Atlanta and on O'ahu, where the Motunui village was built as a physical set, details Disney lays out in its production feature. Big-swing theatrical bets on beloved animated worlds are stacking up industry-wide — see the Super Mario Galaxy theatrical event.
