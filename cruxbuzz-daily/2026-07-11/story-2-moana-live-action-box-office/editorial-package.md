## Verified core facts (Stage 2 summary; figures as of July 11, 2026 — final estimates land Sunday)
- Released July 10, 2026 (Walt Disney Studios). Directed by Thomas Kail (feature debut); screenplay Jared Bush & Dana Ledoux Miller; producers include Dwayne Johnson, Dany Garcia, Hiram Garcia, Beau Flynn, Lin-Manuel Miranda; Auli'i Cravalho is executive producer.
- Cast: Catherine Laga'aia (Moana, film debut), Dwayne Johnson (Maui), John Tui (Chief Tui), Frankie Adams (Sina), Rena Owen (Gramma Tala).
- Music: composer Mark Mancina returns; Lin-Manuel Miranda wrote a new original song, "Along the Way," performed by Laga'aia, Johnson and Cravalho. Soundtrack out June 26, 2026.
- Numbers: $4.5M Thursday previews; ~$17M Friday including previews (trade-reported, approximate); $40M–$45M 3-day projection (down from $60–65M tracking); 3,875 theaters; $250M trade-reported budget before marketing; Rotten Tomatoes ~33–35%, Metacritic 41 (both moving); CinemaScore not yet published.
- Evil Dead Burn (Warner Bros./New Line/Ghost House; dir. Sébastien Vaniček; lead Souheila Yacoub): $2.3M previews, $25–35M projection, ~$15–20M reported budget, RT 72%, R-rated. Franchise-best openings to beat: $25.8M (2013), $24.5M (Evil Dead Rise 2023).
- Context: animated Moana (2016) ~$643M worldwide original run; Moana 2 (Nov 2024) $1.059B worldwide with a $225M five-day Thanksgiving record; remake comparisons — Lilo & Stitch 2025 opened $146M, Little Mermaid 2023 $95.5M, Snow White 2025 $42.2M.
- Casting/culture: Laga'aia is Sydney-born with Samoan heritage (grandparents from Fa'aala, Savai'i and Leulumoega Tuai, 'Upolu), daughter of actor Jay Laga'aia; announced June 2024. Cravalho announced in May 2024 she would not reprise the role. Disney worked again with the Oceanic Cultural Trust. Filming: Atlanta and Hawaii (O'ahu), Jul 29–Nov 22, 2024. Nov 2025 teaser drew 182M views in 24 hours (trade-reported; second among Disney live-action titles behind The Lion King).
- Do NOT use: "10th anniversary release" framing (the 2016 original opened in November, not July); Forbes' "all-time horror RT record" claim for EDB (unverifiable); exact premiere venue (single-source).
