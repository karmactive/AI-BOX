Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce got married at Madison Square Garden on July 3, 2026, in front of roughly 1,000 guests, with Adam Sandler officiating and both spouses in custom Christian Dior Haute Couture by Jonathan Anderson. Five days later, the couple who announced their engagement with the caption "Your English teacher and your gym teacher are getting married" on Taylor Swift's Instagram and Travis Kelce's account still have not released a single official photo. Guests signed NDAs, phones were banned, and the confirmed record comes from a publicist statement, city permits, arena screens that read "JUST&T MARRIED," and teammates' own posts — including a write-up on the San Francisco 49ers' official site about players who attended. Here is everything verified so far, separated from what is still rumor — the same split we applied to other viral moments like the Nick Jonas Prague concert disruption.
