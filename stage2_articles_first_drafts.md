Insurer-level decisions are telling a different story in specific markets — some insurers are adjusting or discontinuing Medicare Advantage offerings in certain counties. A beneficiary in one county may face elimination of their zero-premium plan; a neighbor in the next county may see the same insurer offering more options.

To protect access to your current doctors and avoid potential Part D late-enrollment penalties, actively compare local 2027 plan options and select a replacement before December 7. Plan terminations can also create Special Enrollment Period rights that extend beyond standard AEP timelines — check Medicare.gov or call 1-800-MEDICARE for your specific situation.

## What "Guaranteed Issue" Rights Mean If Your Plan Is Terminated

The distinction between a plan termination and a benefit reduction determines what protections you have.

**Plan termination** — when an insurer exits your county entirely, not just reduces benefits — may trigger Guaranteed Issue rights under federal law. That means you can return to Original Medicare and purchase an eligible standard Medigap supplemental policy without medical underwriting. Private insurers cannot deny coverage or charge higher premiums based on pre-existing conditions. This right is time-limited; for plans terminating December 31, 2026, the window generally runs through 63 days after the termination date. Consult Medicare.gov or a licensed Medicare counselor for the plans available under your qualifying event, as availability varies by circumstance and state.

**Benefit reduction** — the same plan continuing with higher co-pays, stripped supplemental allowances, or a smaller drug formulary — does not trigger Guaranteed Issue rights. You can switch to a different Medicare Advantage plan or a Part D drug plan during AEP, but you cannot purchase Medigap without underwriting.

Your Annual Notice of Change letter outlines every change to premiums, cost-sharing, provider networks, and drug formularies taking effect January 1. Read it before comparing alternatives.

Use the Medicare Plan Finder at medicare.gov — which opens with the full 2027 plan database on October 15 — to compare options in your ZIP code. When comparing plans, check whether your current primary care doctor is in-network for 2027 and whether your specific medications appear on the 2027 formulary at an affordable tier. The headline monthly premium is the least useful starting point.

Millions of Americans on fixed incomes navigating [Medicare's annual enrollment cycle](https://karmactive.com/medicare-annual-enrollment-guide) and [the ongoing shift in Medicare Advantage markets](https://karmactive.com/medicare-advantage-market-trends-2026) face real coverage disruption if they miss the December 7 deadline.

# Judge Permanently Dismisses Felony Case Against Olympian David Hearn Over Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool

A D.C. Superior Court judge has permanently dismissed the criminal case against former Olympic slalom canoeist David Hearn — in a way that prevents prosecutors from ever reviving it. ## What "Dismissed With Prejudice" Actually Means in Practice

Criminal cases end in two ways when prosecutors drop them. A dismissal without prejudice leaves the door open: prosecutors can refile, rework their evidence, or wait for new developments before bringing the same case again. A dismissal with prejudice closes the case permanently. The government cannot refile, cannot seek new indictments on the same facts, and cannot use a different U.S. Attorney to revisit the charges at a later date.
