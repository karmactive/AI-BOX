# Scrubs & Beyond Is Closing Its U.S. Stores: What Shoppers Need to Know

Scrubs & Beyond — one of the largest physical healthcare-apparel retailers in the United States — is closing its stores nationwide. If you rely on a local location for uniform purchases, liquidation sales are currently underway, and the time to use any outstanding gift cards or reward balances is now.

---

## What Is Happening

Current reports confirm that Scrubs & Beyond is shutting its brick-and-mortar retail footprint across the U.S. The company has operated more than 115 stores in 30 states, making this one of the larger specialty retail closures in the healthcare-apparel sector. Liquidation sales are underway at physical locations. The company's online store at scrubsandbeyond.com has been its parallel sales channel. A formal official corporate announcement explaining the reason for the closures was not available in verified primary material at the time of publication. Reports of the closures have been confirmed by multiple current secondary sources.

---

## If You Hold Gift Cards or Store Credit, Act Now

If you hold Scrubs & Beyond gift cards or store credit, act before local retail locations shut their doors permanently. Liquidation sales have begun with all in-store purchases marked final sale, meaning return privileges have ended. Unredeemed store gift cards and loyalty points may lose physical utility once store leases terminate, making it essential to spend your balances online or in-store before official final closure dates are announced.

---

## The End of the Scrub Store: Online Competition Takes Over

The closures mark the near-total exit of dedicated medical-uniform brick-and-mortar retail from U.S. suburban corridors. For years, Scrubs & Beyond occupied a specific niche: nurses, dental hygienists, veterinary technicians, and clinical staff could walk into a store, try on scrubs for fit and feel, and leave the same day with what they needed.

That in-person model has been under sustained pressure from two directions. Direct-to-consumer healthcare apparel brands have grown their online share significantly, selling directly to individual healthcare workers and offering wider size ranges and customization than a physical store can hold in inventory. Hospital and clinic systems have also moved toward bulk procurement contracts, providing branded scrubs directly to staff — removing the individual retail purchase entirely.

For shoppers interested in sustainable textile choices in workwear, the shift also changes what is accessible locally, as [eco-friendly workwear fabric options](https://www.karmactive.com) have largely moved to direct-to-consumer online channels. Healthcare workers transitioning to online purchasing may find it useful to review [practical guidance on navigating retail liquidation sales](https://www.karmactive.com) before spending in the current closeout environment.

---

## What Makes This Closure Unusual

Scrubs & Beyond's own brand materials describe the company as both a major physical retailer — with 115+ stores across 30 states — and one of the largest healthcare-apparel e-commerce businesses in the country. The closures end the physical channel while leaving the e-commerce business intact. This is not a full company shutdown. It is a channel exit.

---

## Is Scrubs & Beyond Closing All Its Stores?

**What happens to my Scrubs & Beyond gift cards and reward points now that stores are closing?**

Gift cards remain redeemable online at scrubsandbeyond.com and at participating physical stores until their final liquidation dates. All merchandise purchased during in-store liquidation sales is classified as final sale — no returns or exchanges. Loyalty reward points should be applied to purchases immediately to avoid expiration.

---

## What to Do Before Stores Close

Check your local store's closure date. Spend outstanding gift card and rewards balances immediately — online or in-store. Expect all in-store purchases to be final sale with no return option. The scrubsandbeyond.com online store is expected to continue operating for uniform purchases after physical locations close.
