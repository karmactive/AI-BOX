The fuel-tank recall addresses a defect allowing fuel vapor or liquid to escape the tank. The second September recall is unusually compact: just 37 vehicles. The potential outcome is not. The incorrect rear axle tail bearings installed during that two-day production window can cause rear-wheel lock-up or driveshaft separation. ### How to Check If Your Ford Is Recalled

Enter your vehicle's 17-character VIN into [NHTSA's free recall lookup tool](https://www.nhtsa.gov/nhtsa-datasets-and-apis). Delays at one hub can propagate through later flights because aircraft and crews operate on interconnected schedules. When West Coast hubs like SFO experience ground delay programs, the ripple effects can extend to distant connections throughout the day.

### The Oil-Inflation-Interest Rate Relationship

The connection between rising oil and falling gold is not obvious from the headlines, but the mechanism is straightforward.

When oil prices rise sharply, headline inflation typically follows. Higher fuel costs feed into transportation, manufacturing, and food costs across the economy. When inflation rises, central banks face pressure to tighten monetary policy. Gold offers no yield, no coupon, and no guaranteed return.

A stronger U.S. dollar can also accompany rate hike expectations, which makes gold more expensive in other currencies and reduces international buying demand.

### What This Means If You Hold Silver

For retail investors who have typically checked Monday morning spot prices to react to the weekend's news, the 24/7 trading window closes a lag that no longer needs to exist. The CME's 24/7 silver futures contract is financially settled, not physically delivered. The 100-ounce contract is cash-settled to the benchmark 5,000-troy-ounce COMEX silver futures contract. [Weekend trades receive the following business day's trade date](https://www.cmegroup.com/notices/electronic-trading/2026/09/20260907.html), with clearing and settlement processed on that business day. The contract is specifically designed for retail participants who want silver price exposure without the logistics of the full benchmark delivery contract.

What this also means is that the contract's weekend price becomes a live signal. An investor in London or Singapore can now trade U.S. silver price risk on a Saturday afternoon in response to a news event that previously would have sat in their portfolio, unhedged, until New York opened on Monday.

Spot silver fell alongside gold as oil prices rallied, inflation data reinforced Federal Reserve rate-hike expectations, and 10-year Treasury yields hit 5%. The debut weekend saw $15 million in silver futures volume accumulate even as prices were declining. That is a useful data point: the 24/7 contract attracted participation in a bearish environment, not just a neutral or bullish one.

### What Is CME's 24/7 Silver Futures Contract?

[CME Group's 100-ounce silver futures contract now trades continuously](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/metals/precious/100-ounce-silver.html) outside of scheduled maintenance. ## ARTICLE 7: 2027 Social Security COLA: What We Know After August Inflation Data

The 2027 Social Security cost-of-living adjustment is not finalized — but August inflation data is now in, and it gives the clearest signal yet. If you receive Social Security benefits and are planning your 2027 household budget, here is what the current data shows, what is still missing, and exactly when the confirmed number will arrive.

### Current Estimates and the Official Announcement Date

The Bureau of Labor Statistics released its August Consumer Price Index data on September 11, 2026. [The CPI-W — the specific inflation measure Social Security uses — rose 3.5% year-over-year](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/2026/). Organizations tracking COLA closely, including The Senior Citizens League (TSCL) and AARP, have projected the 2027 COLA in the range of 3.5% to 3.6%. At an average Social Security retirement benefit of about $2,071 per month, a 3.5% adjustment would add roughly $72 to your monthly check starting in January 2027. [The official 2027 COLA is expected to be announced in October 2026 — the date when BLS releases September CPI data](https://www.ssa.gov/cola/).

### The Part Nobody in the Headlines Is Mentioning

While an estimated 3.5% to 3.6% bump could add roughly $72 to your average monthly benefit starting in January 2027, do not budget that entire amount for living expenses. The federal government automatically deducts Medicare Part B premiums directly from your check before it reaches your account. Any increase in Medicare Part B premiums could reduce the net increase some beneficiaries see in their Social Security payment.

### The CPI-W Methodology — Why the Formula Matters

Social Security's annual adjustment uses the CPI-W — the Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers — which tracks the spending patterns of urban households where a majority of income comes from clerical or wage occupations. Older Americans spend a larger share of their income on medical costs, housing, and prescription drugs — the categories that have outpaced general inflation most consistently. A wage-earner's index can produce a COLA that looks adequate as a percentage while falling short of what retirees actually experience in their monthly expenses.

Medicare Part B premiums compound the gap. Premiums are automatically deducted before deposit. [CMS typically announces the following year's rates in November](https://www.cms.gov/oact/tr/2026). If Part B premiums rise for 2027, some beneficiaries will see part of their Social Security increase offset by higher Medicare deductions.

For beneficiaries looking to plan ahead, [reviewing previous COLA forecasts and Medicare interaction](https://www.karmactive.com/2027-social-security-cola-medicare-taxes/) provides useful context. Additionally, [projections on the size of the 2027 adjustment](https://www.karmactive.com/social-security-cola-2027-forecast/) can help with budget planning, and [the official announcement timeline](https://www.karmactive.com/social-security-cola-2027-announcement-october-forecast-increase/) remains the most useful calendar reference for beneficiaries.

### The Forecast vs. the Official Number

The Social Security Administration's 2026 Trustees Report includes a projected 2027 COLA assumption of 2.4%. That figure is a long-term actuarial planning assumption — it is not a forecast of the actual 2027 COLA and it does not reflect current inflation data. [The actual 2027 adjustment will depend entirely on the three-month average of July, August and September CPI-W](https://www.ssa.gov/OACT/COLA/colasummary.html). The September CPI-W reading is the final input needed to complete the three-month calculation.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**When will the official 2027 Social Security COLA be announced?**

The Social Security Administration will officially announce the 2027 COLA in October 2026. The announcement coincides with the Bureau of Labor Statistics' release of September CPI inflation figures, which provides the final data point required by federal law to calculate the adjustment.

**How is the Social Security COLA calculated?**

[SSA averages the CPI-W readings for July, August and September](https://www.ssa.gov/OACT/TR/TRassum.html) and compares that average with the applicable prior-year three-month average. The resulting percentage increase, rounded as required by law, determines the COLA.

**Will higher Medicare Part B premiums reduce the benefit of the 2027 COLA?**

Yes, for most beneficiaries. Medicare Part B premiums are automatically deducted from Social Security checks before deposit. The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services typically announces the following year's premium rates in November. If Part B premiums increase for 2027, that amount reduces the net dollar gain from the COLA before any money reaches your account.

**When will the 2027 Social Security increase appear in checks?**

The increase will first appear in Social Security payments issued in January 2027, according to each beneficiary's regular payment schedule.

### What to Do Until October 14

Current forecasts of 3.5% to 3.6% are estimates based on two of the three months of CPI-W data needed for the official calculation. They are useful as planning guides — not as the confirmed amount. The official 2027 Social Security COLA will be confirmed in October 2026. Return on that date for the verified figure, the net-benefit calculation after Medicare deductions, and the implications for January 2027 deposits.
