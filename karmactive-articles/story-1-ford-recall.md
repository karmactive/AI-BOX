# Ford Recalls 223,472 Vehicles Over Fuel-Tank Risk: Check Your VIN

If you own a recent Ford Explorer, Bronco, or F-150, a federal safety recall may affect your vehicle. Two separate NHTSA campaigns filed in September 2026 cover more than 223,000 vehicles for a fuel-tank defect that creates a fire risk — plus a second, smaller campaign targeting just 37 F-150 trucks over a potentially severe axle problem.

---

## What Ford Recalled

The larger of the two campaigns covers 223,472 vehicles and addresses a fuel-tank defect. According to NHTSA filings, the defect can allow fuel vapor or liquid to leak from the tank — a hazard near hot exhaust components underneath the vehicle. The second campaign covers only 37 specifically-built 2026 Ford F-150 trucks, produced over a narrow two-day window: May 27 and 28, 2026. Those vehicles received incorrect rear axle tail bearings. Incorrect bearings are not a cosmetic problem — they can cause rear-wheel lock-up or driveshaft separation while driving, both serious crash hazards. In both cases, Ford is required to repair the vehicles at no cost under federal law. Owner notification letters for the axle campaign are expected on or around September 21, 2026. VIN check availability for this campaign began September 14, 2026.

---

## Which Vehicles Are Affected — and What Owners Should Do

Ford owners should not assume that a recall headline means every vehicle of that model is affected. Recall campaigns apply to specific vehicles, and NHTSA's VIN lookup is the most useful way to confirm whether a particular vehicle needs repair. For affected vehicles, the repairs are provided without charge through the manufacturer or dealer. Owners should check the VIN rather than relying only on the model name or model year.

---

## What Ford Dealers Must Do — and Warning Signs to Watch For

The fuel-tank recall addresses a defect allowing fuel vapor or liquid to escape the tank. If you detect a gasoline smell inside or underneath your vehicle, NHTSA advises parking outdoors away from structures and contacting your Ford dealership directly for priority inspection. You do not need to wait for a mailed owner notification letter — NHTSA's portal allows you to check right now.

Ford has been managing multiple overlapping recall campaigns across its model line throughout 2026. Earlier this year, [Ford simultaneously filed three separate recalls covering roughly 160,000 vehicles, including 148,663 Mustangs](https://www.karmactive.com/ford-three-recalls-160000-vehicles-2026/). A separate campaign covered [565,000 Broncos over an unrelated engine fire risk tied to wiring defects](https://www.karmactive.com/ford-bronco-recall-engine-fire-wiring-defect-2026/). And owners of certain Maverick and Bronco Sport vehicles previously received ["Do Not Drive" orders over ball joint concerns](https://www.karmactive.com/ford-maverick-bronco-sport-do-not-drive-recall-2026-ball-joint/). For anyone with multiple Ford vehicles, the only reliable check is the VIN lookup — model name and model year alone do not confirm whether a specific vehicle is inside a recall campaign.

---

## The 37-Truck F-150 Campaign: Small but Serious

The second September recall is unusually compact: just 37 vehicles. The potential outcome is not. The incorrect rear axle tail bearings installed during that two-day production window can cause rear-wheel lock-up or driveshaft separation. Either event while driving creates a crash risk for the driver and for other vehicles on the road. The narrow production window — May 27 and 28, 2026 — means the defect was traced to a specific supplier batch rather than a broad manufacturing process.

---

## How to Check If Your Ford Is Recalled

**How do I check if my Ford vehicle is included in the recall?**

Enter your vehicle's 17-character VIN into NHTSA's free recall lookup tool at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall applies only to the specific vehicles identified in that campaign — not to every vehicle of the same model or model year. If your VIN shows an open recall, NHTSA says repairs must be performed at no cost at an authorized Ford dealership.
