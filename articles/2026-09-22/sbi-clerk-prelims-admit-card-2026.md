The SBI Clerk Prelims admit card for 2026 is out. If you are appearing for the Junior Associate examination, you need to download your hall ticket now — the Preliminary Examination is scheduled for **September 26 and 27, 2026**, and you cannot enter the exam centre without a printed copy.

## How to Download Your SBI Clerk 2026 Admit Card

1. Go to **sbi.bank.in** — the official SBI careers portal.
2. Navigate to the Careers section and find the Junior Associate (Customer Support and Sales) 2026 recruitment listing.
3. Click the **Preliminary Examination Call Letter** download link.
4. Log in using your registration number and date of birth (or password, depending on your login type).
5. Download and save the PDF. Print at least one copy before the exam.

Use only the official SBI portal. Third-party sites offering direct admit card downloads are not authorised — do not enter your registration credentials on any site other than sbi.bank.in.

## What to Carry to the Exam

- Printed admit card (a digital-only copy on a phone is not a substitute — bring a paper printout).
- A valid government-issued photo ID: Aadhaar, PAN card, passport, voter ID, or driving licence.
- The ID must match the name on your admit card exactly.

## Check These Details the Moment You Download

Open your admit card and verify:
- Your full name (spelling must match your application)
- Exam date and reporting time
- Exam centre name and address
- Photograph and signature

If any detail is wrong, contact SBI immediately at the helpline listed on the official SBI careers page. Do not wait until exam day to raise a discrepancy — mismatches can bar entry at the gate.

## What Happens If You Miss the Prelims?

The Preliminary Examination is a qualifying round. Candidates who pass advance to the Mains. There is no provision to appear for Mains without clearing Prelims, and the exam is held over two days (September 26 and 27) with candidates divided by shift. Your admit card will specify which date and shift applies to you.

From SBI's official careers portal at sbi.bank.in. Go to the Junior Associate (Customer Support and Sales) 2026 recruitment section and click the Preliminary Examination Call Letter link. Log in with your registration number and date of birth. Do not download from third-party sites.

The SBI Clerk Prelims window is narrow — four days from now. Download your admit card today, confirm every detail, and arrange a printout before September 26.
