## STEP 2 — Competitive Coverage Scan & Missing-Points Analysis

**What mainstream coverage (Euronews, Al Jazeera, CNN, AccuWeather, Yale Climate Connections, CBS, Time, Fortune) is already reporting:**
- Death toll figures (1,300+ WHO Europe-wide estimate; ~1,000 in France; regional Spain estimates)
- Temperature records in Spain/France/Italy
- WHO's "dress rehearsal" warning
- The cultural/infrastructure debate over air conditioning in Europe
- Wildfire risk in Italy/Balkans, school closures, red alerts

**Gaps found and integrated into the draft (for E-E-A-T + SEO/AEO depth):**
1. WHO's separate, longer-horizon statistic — **200,000+ heat deaths across the EU over the past 4 years**, and that **most were preventable** (WHO/Europe, 11 June 2026 statement) — rarely tied together with the June event in general coverage.
2. WHO's finding that **existing adaptation measures already cut heat deaths by an estimated 80%** — a solutions-oriented data point.
3. WHO's finding that **more than half of European Region countries still lack a comprehensive heat-health action plan** — accountability angle missing from most consumer coverage.
4. Exact **country-by-country AC ownership rates** (IEA): Germany 3%, UK 5%, France 25%, Spain ~40%, Italy ~50%, EU average ~20% vs. US ~90% — most articles cite only the EU-wide 20% figure.
5. IEA's forward projection: EU air-conditioner stock **more than doubling to ~275 million units by 2050**.
6. **World Weather Attribution's precise attribution numbers** — the heatwave would have been "virtually impossible" in 1976; ~3.5°C hotter than the same pattern would have been 50 years ago; overnight heat now ~100x more likely than in 2003.
7. The **overnight/"tropical night" danger** — record-warm nightly minimums in Spain (20.14°C/19.81°C) and France (22.0°C average minimum) that deny the body recovery time; under-covered medically relevant detail.
8. **Barcelona's Climate Shelters Network** (500+ sites, 99.2% of residents within a 10-minute walk, new "microrefugios" in neighborhood pharmacies/shops) — a concrete, replicable local solution, under-covered in English-language press.
9. WHO's direct **public safety guidance** (hydration, clothing, checking on isolated neighbours, when to call a doctor/ambulance) — practical, actionable, rarely reproduced in full.
10. WMO's independent confirmation that Europe is warming at **more than twice the global average**, and its forecast that global temperatures will likely **stay at or near record levels through 2029** — long-term/sustainability framing.

All of the above are woven into the article body as plain factual statements (no "here's what's missing" framing), each backed by a first-hand source below.
