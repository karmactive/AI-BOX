The Number FIFA Isn't Counting

Beyond the 9 million tonnes generated by the tournament itself, FIFA's commercial arrangement with Aramco — the world's largest corporate fossil fuel producer — is estimated by researchers to normalise demand for oil that could induce an additional 30 million tonnes of CO₂ equivalent. That is more than three times the direct tournament footprint, and it does not appear in any FIFA sustainability document. The Swiss advertising regulator rejected FIFA's "carbon neutral" claim for Qatar 2022. The pattern continues.

The Northeast Corridor

Amtrak's Northeast Corridor connects Boston, New York, Philadelphia, and Washington D.C. — all 2026 host cities or nearby. Rail emits roughly 80% less CO₂ per passenger than a domestic flight on the same route.

Cluster Your Matches

Selecting games in geographically close cities cuts the travel distance dramatically.

Tap for details →

Geographic Pairing

Vancouver + Seattle area, New York + Boston, or Mexico City + Guadalajara are geographically compact combinations. Avoiding transcontinental multi-city itineraries is the single biggest lever for fans.

🌱

Verified Removal

Not all carbon programmes are equal. Removal differs from offsetting.

Tap for details →

Removal, Not Offsets

Carbon offset credits — particularly forestry-based ones — have faced widespread criticism for overestimated impact. Verified carbon dioxide removal (direct air capture, biochar, enhanced weathering) actually removes CO₂ already emitted. Look for providers certified under independent frameworks.

👥

Group Travel

Sharing transport and accommodation divides your personal share of emissions.

Tap for details →

Share the Load

A hire car shared among four people produces roughly one-quarter of the per-person emissions versus solo driving. Shared accommodation concentrates the accommodation footprint. Organised supporters' coaches or rail group bookings are the most efficient ground options for large fan groups.
