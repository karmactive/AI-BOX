Now

Where We Are: ~1.44°C (2025 recorded)

The WMO confirmed 2025 as the third warmest year on record. 2024 was the first calendar year above 1.5°C.

🔥
Extreme Heat
Europe's May 2026 heatwave — the earliest on record — killed people in France and the UK. Kew Gardens hit 34.8°C in May.
🧊
Ice & Glaciers
Arctic sea ice hits record lows. The Southwest U.S. heatwave of early 2026 was called "one of the six most astonishing weather events of the century" by scientists.
🐧
Species Loss
IUCN classified emperor penguins and Antarctic fur seals as Endangered in 2026, directly linked to low sea-ice levels caused by warming.
🌊
Coral Reefs
70–90% of the world's coral reefs are projected to be gone at 1.5°C sustained warming. We are already inside that corridor.

The Cost Paid by Environmental Defenders

On June 4 — the day before World Environment Day — the UN's human rights office (OHCHR) reported that nearly 600 environmental and land defenders had been killed or disappeared worldwide over the past three years, in at least 55 countries. In at least 55 countries, activists have been detained for their environmental work. Those numbers arrived the day before the world gathered to celebrate the planet.

#NowForClimate

From Watching to Doing

The Climate Limbo is a dance challenge — but beyond the trend, UNEP's #NowForClimate campaign names specific actions for individuals, cities, and governments.

💃
Join the Climate Dance Challenge
Film yourself doing the Climate Limbo to DJ Alok's "Deep Down," post with #NowForClimate, and tag @UNEP on TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube.
🌱
Cut Food Waste
Food systems account for ~30% of global greenhouse gas emissions (UNEP 2026). Roughly one-third of all food produced is wasted. Reducing waste at home is one of the highest-impact actions available to households.
🏙️
Demand Urban Heat Action
Ask your local government if they have a heat action plan. WHO's new Heat-Health Action Plans guidance launches June 11, 2026 — with an 8-element framework cities can follow.
🌳
Support Nature-Based Solutions
Mangroves store carbon at up to 18× the rate of typical forests. Peatlands hold over 30% of all soil-stored carbon on just 3% of land. Protecting these ecosystems can deliver up to 30% of the mitigation needed by 2050 (IPCC).
⚡
Switch Where You Can
Clean energy is now cheaper in most of the world than new fossil fuel capacity. In 2025, 1 in 4 new cars sold globally was electric. The economics of the energy transition have fundamentally shifted.
📢
Defend the Defenders
Nearly 600 environmental defenders have been killed or disappeared in 3 years. Supporting legal aid organizations and freedom-of-expression campaigns protects the people on the frontlines of climate protection.
