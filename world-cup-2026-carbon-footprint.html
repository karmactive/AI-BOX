SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, seats up to 70,000 and is hosting 8 World Cup 2026 matches — including the U.S. opener on June 12. With 6 million tickets in circulation across 16 cities, fan travel to and from venues like this one accounts for nearly nine in every ten tonnes of the tournament's projected carbon output. 6 Million Tickets
Up from 3.4M in Qatar. Nearly twice the spectators, now travelling across a continent three times larger.
🛢️
Aramco Sponsorship
A $400M FIFA deal with Saudi Aramco — responsible for over 4% of global CO₂ — is estimated to induce 30M additional tonnes CO₂e.
🏟️
Existing Venues
Stadium construction is only 3.1% of the footprint — down from 24.6% for Qatar, where 7 new stadiums were built.
⚖️
Qatar Ruling
A Swiss advertising regulator in 2023 ruled FIFA's "carbon-neutral" claim for Qatar 2022 was "unsubstantiated."
🚗
6.5M Cars/Year
The SGR report equates the tournament's footprint to 6.5 million average British cars driven for an entire year.
🌱
Vancouver Leads
Vancouver topped sustainability rankings with an Eco Score of 6.7/10, and 9/10 for greenhouse gas emissions.

Key Moments: From FIFA's Climate Promises to 2026
2014
Brazil World Cup: ~2.72M tonnes CO₂e. Deforestation and social disruption tied to infrastructure.
2018
Russia World Cup: ~2.16M tonnes CO₂e. Smallest footprint in recent editions.
2021 – COP26
FIFA commits to net-zero by 2040 and 50% emissions cut by 2030 at COP26 climate summit.
2022
Qatar World Cup: ~3.63M tonnes CO₂e. FIFA calls it the first "carbon-neutral" tournament.
2023
Swiss advertising regulator rules Qatar 2022 "carbon-neutral" claim was unsubstantiated. First major ruling against FIFA greenwashing.
2024
FIFA signs $400M, 4-year sponsorship with Saudi Aramco. 130 women players from 27 nations sign open letter opposing the deal.
June 2026
Tournament begins. SGR and Greenly publish independent carbon analyses. Sierra Club organises protests at World Cup venues.
