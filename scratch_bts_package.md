## 2. ARTICLE TEXT (no H1; ready for WordPress blocks)

**[INTRO]**

All seven members of BTS confirmed in late July 2026 that they will not submit their music for consideration at the 69th Grammy Awards, scheduled for February 7, 2027, in Los Angeles. RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V and Jungkook posted the same statement on their individual Instagram accounts, saying the group had decided together to sit out this year's Grammy process.

The move follows the Recording Academy's addition of five new award categories, laid out in its official [announcement of the 2027 Grammys' new categories and rule updates](https://www.grammy.com/news/2027-grammys-new-categories-rule-updates/), among them Best Asian Pop Music Performance. The category calls for "meaningful use" of an Asian language, a rule that would leave out BTS's two biggest English-language songs of 2026.

The decision lands months after BTS released their fifth studio album, ARIRANG, their first full group project since completing mandatory military service in South Korea. CruxBuzz's earlier report on the [2026 Grammy nominations and its own new categories](https://cruxbuzz.com/2026-grammys-nominations-kendrick-lamar-leads-9-95-categories-two-new-cbs-paramount-plus/) covered how the ceremony's category list has been shifting year over year.

**[MIDDLE BLOCK 1 — The new category]**

Best Asian Pop Music Performance is one of five categories added for the 69th Grammy Awards, alongside Best Latin Song, Best Traditional Pop Vocal Performance, Best R&B Collaboration Or Duo/Group Performance and Best Traditional Folk Album. In a separate interview posted on [grammy.com with Recording Academy CEO Harvey Mason Jr.](https://www.grammy.com/news/grammys-ceo-harvey-mason-jr-2027-grammys-interview-new-categories/), the new category is described as recognizing pop recordings from or widely tied to Asian markets, including K-pop, J-pop and C-pop, that make meaningful use of an Asian language.

That wording matters for BTS. Their 2026 single "Swim" opened at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100, and "Normal" was another major single from ARIRANG, and both songs are mostly or fully in English. As written, the rule would keep both out of the new category, even though ARIRANG itself was one of the year's biggest album releases from an Asian pop act, a story CruxBuzz has followed as part of its [wider music industry coverage](https://cruxbuzz.com/category/entertainment/music/).

**[MIDDLE BLOCK 2 — BTS and HYBE's reasoning]**

In their joint Instagram statement, BTS wrote: "I hope that music can be heard and loved for what it is, rather than being divided by region or language." The same wording appeared on all seven members' accounts, a format the group has used for past joint announcements.

HYBE, the group's label, followed with a statement to Korean media framing the choice as BTS's own. A HYBE representative said the decision was "not a boycott at the company level" and confirmed other HYBE artists who want to submit music for the 69th Grammy Awards would continue with their entries as planned. The label added that neither BTS nor HYBE was asking other acts to follow the same path. For readers tracking how other K-pop acts are performing on the charts this year, CruxBuzz's [coverage of Rosé and Bruno Mars' "APT" run atop the Global 200](https://cruxbuzz.com/apt-tops-billboard-global-200-for-fourth-week-rose-and-bruno-mars-hit-146m-streams-breaking-k-pop-solo-and-global-chart-records/) has more on that wider chart picture.

**[MIDDLE BLOCK 3 — Recording Academy's response and ARIRANG's chart run]**

Recording Academy CEO Harvey Mason Jr. responded directly to BTS's decision, saying he was "saddened to hear that BTS has chosen not to participate in the Grammy Awards process this year," while adding, "as a music creator, I understand and respect their decision." He also said recognition in a genre category and recognition in the Grammys' General Field are "not mutually exclusive" and that "an artist can absolutely pursue both."

The backdrop to the decision is ARIRANG's chart run. The album opened atop the Billboard 200 with a reported 641,000 equivalent album units, the largest week for a group album since Billboard began measuring units in December 2014, according to [Billboard's own list of BTS's Hot 100 and Billboard 200 No. 1s](https://www.billboard.com/lists/bts-number-ones-hot-100-billboard-200/). It was BTS's seventh Billboard 200 chart-topper and came after years away while members completed military service.

**[CONCLUSION]**

BTS's decision not to submit music for the 69th Grammy Awards was announced by all seven members on Instagram in late July 2026. The group's stated reasoning was included in their joint post: a hope that music can be heard and loved for what it is, rather than divided by region or language. HYBE issued a separate statement describing the decision as the group's own rather than a company-wide position, and noted other HYBE artists would proceed with their own Grammy submissions.

The new Best Asian Pop Music Performance category was covered in the Recording Academy's own announcement of five new categories for the 69th Grammy Awards, along with the category's language requirement. Recording Academy CEO Harvey Mason Jr.'s response was also addressed, including his statement that he was saddened by the decision but respected it, and his comments on the relationship between genre categories and the General Field.

ARIRANG's chart performance was also part of the story, with the album's Billboard 200 debut and BTS's history of Hot 100 and Billboard 200 No. 1s referenced in Billboard's own chart data. The 69th Grammy Awards are scheduled to air February 7, 2027.
