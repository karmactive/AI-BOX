## Extracted facts (from the three sources)
- The National Hurricane Center (NHC) is tracking 3 systems with development potential in the Atlantic.
- System 1 (North/Western Atlantic, near Bermuda): disorganized storms near Bermuda, low odds (~10% over 48 hrs), drifts east into cooler water, window closes Wednesday. No threat to land / U.S.
- System 2 (lower tropical Atlantic tropical wave near Leeward Islands): ~10% chance, runs into hostile wind shear, forecast to dissipate.
- System 3 (Eastern Atlantic low pressure, far eastern Atlantic): medium odds; 20% over 2 days, jumps to 60% over next 7 days. A tropical wave over Africa merges with it in a few days. A tropical depression could form by later this week. Must navigate Saharan Air (dust).
- Latest computer model guidance: none of these systems threatening the U.S. at this time.
- Next names on list: Cristobal and Dolly.
- We're on the verge of the busiest part of hurricane season (mid-August through late October); ~1 month until peak (Sept 10).
