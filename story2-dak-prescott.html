Dak Prescott signed a four-year, $240 million contract extension with the Dallas Cowboys — one of the most valuable deals in NFL history at the time. He proposed to Sarah Jane Ramos on a golf course in October 2024. They had two daughters together. They had 250 wedding guests. The venue was Lake Como, Italy. The date was April 10, 2026. None of it happened.

In March 2026, before the ceremony, the wedding was called off. Sarah Jane Ramos, who had been with Prescott since the fall of 2023, confirmed the split herself on Instagram — and directly denied that a prenup was involved. What she did describe, through representatives and her own posts, was a matter of trust. Prescott had reportedly been using private, hidden social media profiles to contact other women throughout their relationship. A custody filing followed. Then came an unexpected custody agreement reached outside court on April 15. Two daughters — Margaret Jane Rose (MJ), born February 2024, and Aurora Rayne, born May 2025 — are at the center of an ongoing co-parenting arrangement. This is what the documented record shows.

What the Record Shows
Key factual details from court filings, official statements, and verified reports
What Exactly Were the "Incognito Social Media Profiles"? ▼
Reports indicate Prescott had created and used private social media profiles — separate from his public accounts — to communicate with other women, including ex-girlfriends, during the relationship. Ramos had reportedly become aware of this conduct during the relationship and remained in it before ultimately making the decision to end the engagement. A representative confirmed this was the underlying issue in the split.
What Is the Custody Arrangement? ▼
The temporary custody settlement reached on April 15, 2026 established joint decision-making for both daughters and includes travel restrictions. It was agreed outside of the formal court process as a private interim arrangement. A formal court hearing remains on the calendar. Both Prescott and Ramos have stated publicly that their priority is raising their daughters together in a positive environment.
Did a Prenup Play a Role? ▼
Sarah Jane Ramos directly and publicly denied the prenup narrative on Instagram, writing: "This had nothing to do with a prenup." Her post was a response to widespread speculation online that a prenuptial agreement had triggered the split. The statement was clear and unambiguous — the issue, according to Ramos and her representatives, was the conduct described as "ongoing infidelity."
What About the Dallas Cowboys Mansion? ▼
Following the split, reports emerged that Prescott made a decision regarding the Dallas-area property the couple had been associated with. Details of the property arrangement were addressed in later reporting, though neither Prescott nor Ramos issued formal statements on the specific real estate situation. As of May 2026, both parties have kept property details out of public statements.
What Is Dak Prescott's Contract Situation with the Cowboys? ▼
Prescott signed a four-year, $240 million extension with the Dallas Cowboys that includes $231 million in guarantees and an $80 million signing bonus, making it one of the most financially significant quarterback deals in NFL history at the time of signing. His 2026 cap hit is estimated above $74 million, according to contract tracking at Over The Cap. In the 2025 NFL season, Prescott completed 67 percent of his passes for 4,552 yards with a 30:10 touchdown-to-interception ratio.
