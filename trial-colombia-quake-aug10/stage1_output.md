Choc¢ is one of Earth's highest-rainfall regions. This persistent soil saturation causes liquefaction during earthquakes-a phenomenon where water-saturated soil loses bearing strength and behaves like liquid, causing foundations to sink, underground utilities to rupture, and roads to buckle. In a region with minimal paved infrastructure, this secondary hazard can isolate communities from rescue teams for days.
