KEY FACTS (primary: USGS; secondary: CNN/AP/Caracol Radio):
- 7.4 magnitude earthquake, Monday Aug 10 2026, 7:34 a.m. local (8:34 a.m. ET).
- Epicenter: San José del Palmar, department of Chocó, ~280 km (173 mi) west of Bogotá. Depth 107 km (USGS).
- Felt strongly in Bogotá (alarms, evacuations to streets). Strong shaking in Cali, Pereira, Armenia, Manizales, Zarzal.
- Chocó governor Nubia Carolina Córdoba Curi: injured people + severe building damage in Quibdó (regional capital); concern about aftershocks.
- Nóvita mayor Jaime Velásquez (Caracol Radio): "strongest in our entire history"; health center overwhelmed; church + many buildings badly damaged; no deaths reported in Nóvita yet.
- USGS: >2 million people exposed to very strong shaking (MMI level 7/10) — "considerable damage in poorly built structures," slight-moderate in well-built.
- USGS PAGER: "significant casualties" possible; ~1-in-3 chance fatalities top 1,000.
- President Abelardo de la Espriella convened emergency committee; heading to Bogotá; will lead from UNGRD (National Unit for Disaster Risk Management).
- At least 2 dead in Manizales (Mayor Jorge Eduardo Rojas): buildings totally/partially destroyed; aerial cable car all 3 lines out, Line 3 evacuating.
- Pereira: 4-story building collapsed (fruit stalls below); Iglesia San José facade partial collapse.
- Colombian Army: 5 platoons of rescue soldiers deployed.
- USGS event ID: us6000tjl2.
