- **Answer key manipulation**: Official answer keys changed post-exam to match organized cheating ring's answers.

If Jharkhand's fraud involves official collusion, the credibility of all recent JPSC/JSSC exams is compromised. Every job filled under suspect exam results could be invalidated.

## Adivasi job access and reservation quotas

Jharkhand's population is 28% Adivasi. State law (and Indian Constitution Article 46) mandates job reservation for Scheduled Tribes (Adivasis) in government recruitment.

If exam fraud systematically excluded qualified Adivasi candidates, it violated their constitutional rights. Evidence would be:

## Government remedy options

Remedies available to Jharkhand government:

1. **Re-examination**: Affected candidates retake exams under enhanced security. Cost: high; implementation: 6+ months.
2. **Invalidation + refilling**: Annul fraudulent exams; recruit from waitlisted candidates; reopen positions. Uncertainty: who is truly qualified vs. who cheated?
3. **Judicial inquiry**: External judge investigates; findings inform remedial action. Credibility: higher.
4. **Criminal prosecution**: Pursue charges against officials and fraud ring members; recoveries (if any) partially offset state costs.

## What systemic failure looks like

Jharkhand's public sector recruitment is now under cloud. Candidates who cleared exams years ago may face questions: Did I really earn this job? Employers of state employees wonder: Are these people qualified?

The damage extends beyond jobs. It erodes institutional credibility. If exams are fixed, people lose faith in meritocracy. Adivasis, who depend on reservation protections within an otherwise merit-based system, are double-hit: their reserved seats become suspect in public perception.
