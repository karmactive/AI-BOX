## Systematic Threats Against Reservation Commentary

The targeting of Santy Sharma may reflect a broader pattern of online harassment against individuals who publicly discuss India's reservation system.

Reservation is a constitutionally protected affirmative action program that generates ongoing debate. Some voices argue reservations are necessary to remedy historical discrimination; others argue they should be reformed or phased out. Both positions are legitimate political speech protected by Article 19(1)(a) of the Indian Constitution (freedom of speech).

## Digital Rights and Silencing Effects

Digital rights advocates have flagged a pattern in platform moderation: creators facing harassment sometimes have their platforms revoked, and the creators—not the harassers—bear the consequence.
