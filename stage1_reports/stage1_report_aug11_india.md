## Elaboration Notes

**Air India — Deep dive needed on:** The discrepancy between Air India's initial "turbulence" statement and sources confirming triple hydraulic failure. The post-flight maintenance report shows 9 warning messages in one minute. The A320neo has 3 independent hydraulic systems — all three affected is extremely rare. Pilot's manual nose-down input during stall recovery caused the 300-ft loss. DGCA psychoactive screening protocol triggered (standard procedure after serious incidents).

**H1N1 — Environmental angle:** Monsoon-driven temperature drop increases viral shedding and indoor congregation. H3N2 co-circulation creates "twindemic" risk. 69% household symptom reporting suggests community spread beyond clinical cases.

**Allahabad HC — Constitutional law angle:** Article 25 (freedom of religion) vs Article 21 (personal liberty). The court's joint liability order (father + public servant) is unusual — needs legal expert commentary.

**Santy Sharma — Digital rights angle:** YouTube's opaque content moderation during political controversies. Pattern of account suspensions coinciding with sensitive social media discussions. Need clarification on whether channel reinstatement appeal filed.

**Meta — Regulatory escalation:** This is the first time a parliamentary committee has threatened safe harbor withdrawal for a CEO personally. Precedent: similar pressure on Twitter/X earlier. Timeline: Aug 2 removal → Aug 5-6 MeitY meetings → Aug 7-8 panel ultimatum → Aug 11 apology deadline.

**Jharkhand — Adivasi lens:** Jharkhand has 28%+ tribal population. Educational access barriers (language, infrastructure, economic). The 14th JPSC exam controversy ties to broader governance questions in tribal-majority states.
