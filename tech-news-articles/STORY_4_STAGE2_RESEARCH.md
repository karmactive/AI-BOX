# STORY 4: VERIZON-GOOGLE PARTNERSHIP - STAGE 2 RESEARCH & ENHANCEMENT

## KEY FINDINGS

**Deal Details:**
- Amount: $1 billion+
- Announcement: July 24, 2026 (Verizon Q2 earnings call)
- Type: Dark fiber connectivity agreement
- Duration: Multi-year commitment
- Purpose: Connect Google's AI data centers
- Future: Verizon expects multiple additional deals worth billions more

**What is Dark Fiber?**
- Installed fiber-optic infrastructure
- Customer (Google) operates with their own equipment
- Gives customer direct control over capacity and design
- Not a managed service (customer manages everything)
- Google avoids paying carrier management fees
