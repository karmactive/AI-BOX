# STORY 3: SAMSUNG GALAXY Z FOLD 8 - STAGE 2 RESEARCH & ENHANCEMENT

## RESEARCH FINDINGS

**Key Details from Research:**
- Announcement: July 22, 2026 (Galaxy Unpacked event)
- Availability: August 5, 2026
- Key Innovation: First "wide-screen" foldable (4:3 aspect ratio)
- Display: 5.5" cover screen, 7.6" main display (new proportions)
- Processor: Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5
- Battery: 4,800 mAh, 45W wired, 20W wireless charging
- Water/Dust: IP48 rating (improved)
- Glass: Gorilla Glass Ceramic 3 on cover display
- Storage/RAM: Up to 1024GB, 16GB RAM
- OS: Android 17, One UI 9
- Price (US 256GB): $1,900
- Price (EU 256GB): €2,000

**Notable Features:**
- Reduced crease visibility with improved hinge
- Landscape-oriented form factor
- Wider cover screen for one-handed use (tablet look when unfolded)
- Z Fold 8 Ultra variant also available
