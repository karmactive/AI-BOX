- Major cloud providers experience outages periodically
- Microsoft's scale means outages affect millions globally
- Redundancy systems sometimes cascade rather than isolate failures
- Configuration management critical for preventing incidents

**Lessons Learned:**
- Backup communication channels important for outages

- Deployment processes need better validation
