Punjab Lecturer Cadre admit card 2026 released for 1,013 posts — download hall ticket before exam

Admit cards for the Punjab Lecturer Cadre recruitment examination 2026 are now available for download. Candidates applying for any of the 1,013 posts should download their hall ticket from the official Education Recruitment Board portal before the examination begins.

The admit card is available at erd.punjab.gov.in — the official ERD Punjab website. Candidates should use their registration credentials to log in and download their hall ticket.

**What to check on your admit card**

Once downloaded, verify the following details before the examination date:

- Your full name (as registered)
- Roll number
- Examination centre name and address
- Date and session of your paper
- Photograph and signature

If any detail is incorrect, contact the Education Recruitment Board before the examination date. Do not attend the examination with an admit card showing incorrect information without first seeking correction.

**What to carry on examination day**

Bring both your printed admit card and a valid photo ID to the examination centre. Accepted forms of ID typically include an Aadhaar card, driving licence, or passport. Centres will not allow entry without both documents. Carry the admit card printed clearly — blurred or illegible prints may be rejected at the gate.

**Examination dates — important verification note**

A discrepancy exists in published examination dates across media sources.

Times of India has reported examination dates of September 19, 20, 26, and 27, 2026.

Jagran Josh has reported dates of September 19, 20, 25, and 26, 2026.

The difference is in the later dates: one source gives September 25 and 26, the other gives September 26 and 27. Before relying on either date set, candidates should check the official ERD Punjab admit card directly. The hall ticket itself will carry the authoritative examination date and session for each candidate. Do not rely solely on third-party reports — check your individual admit card.

**About the recruitment**

The Punjab Lecturer Cadre 2026 recruitment covers 1,013 posts across multiple subject areas. This is one of the larger state-level lecturer recruitment cycles in Punjab's recent recruitment calendar. The examination is being administered by the Education Recruitment Board (ERD), Punjab.

The final examination schedule and any updates will be posted on the ERD Punjab official website. Download your admit card now from erd.punjab.gov.in and confirm your examination date directly from the document.
