## Why Toxicology Still Pending Matters

The most important thing to understand about this case right now is what toxicology actually determines. A toxicology report identifies what substances were present in a person's body at the time of death and at what levels. Until that report is finalized and reviewed by the coroner or medical examiner, the cause of death cannot be officially determined.

Reports circulating about a fentanyl-laced pill should be treated as investigative claims under active examination — not as the established cause of death. The official record will be the toxicology findings combined with any formal announcement from the Greenville authorities or the coroner's office.

For families and fans who have encountered these reports on social media and entertainment sites, that distinction matters. A claim being investigated is not the same as a claim being confirmed.
