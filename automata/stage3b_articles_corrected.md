## What "Guaranteed Issue" Rights Mean If Your Plan Is Terminated

If your insurer terminates your plan completely — not simply reduces benefits, but exits your county — federal law may grant you a Guaranteed Issue right. Read your Annual Notice of Change letter to determine which applies to you. Your ANOC outlines every change to premiums, cost-sharing, provider networks, and drug formularies taking effect January 1.

## What "Dismissed With Prejudice" Actually Means

Criminal cases can be dismissed in two ways. A dismissal without prejudice leaves the door open for prosecutors to refile charges, rework their evidence, or wait for new developments before bringing the same case again. A dismissal with prejudice bars the government from refiling the same case. It closes the case permanently. The government cannot refile, cannot seek new indictments on the same facts, and cannot use a future administration or a different U.S. Attorney to revisit the charges.

The procedural context matters here. Prosecutors reportedly sought a dismissal without prejudice — which would have preserved their ability to refile — and the judge rejected that path. The ruling with prejudice was a deliberate judicial choice, not a default outcome.
