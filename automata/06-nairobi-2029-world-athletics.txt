Nairobi wins 2029 World Athletics Championships in African first as London bid falls short

Nairobi has been selected to host the 2029 World Athletics Championships, making Kenya's capital the first African city to stage the event in its 49-year history. The decision was announced on September 15, 2026, with Munich separately confirmed as the host for the 2031 edition.

London had submitted a major competing bid and was widely considered a strong contender. The choice of Nairobi over one of the world's most recognised athletics venues is a notable shift for a sport that has held its flagship championship primarily in North America, Europe, and Asia.

**Why this decision matters beyond the headline**

Africa has produced some of the most dominant athletes in the history of the World Athletics Championships. Distance runners from Kenya and Ethiopia have won a disproportionate share of medals across decades of competition. The championships have never been held on the continent where so many of its champions come from.

Hosting the event in Nairobi in 2029 changes that. It also gives the sport an opportunity to build its audience in a region where athletics is genuinely popular at a grassroots level, rather than a niche spectator sport.

For Kenya specifically, this is a major infrastructure and tourism moment. Reuters confirmed that Kasarani Stadium in Nairobi is being renovated ahead of the 2029 event. Kasarani has a history with major athletics — it hosted the 1987 All-Africa Games — but staging the World Athletics Championships requires a significantly higher standard of facility and logistics than any previous event held there.

**What happened to London's bid**

London's bid was backed by the existing infrastructure of the London Stadium, one of the few venues in the world capable of hosting a major championships at short notice without significant renovation. The city hosted the 2017 World Championships, which drew strong attendance and broad media coverage.

The Guardian confirmed that London was passed over in favour of Nairobi. No official explanation for the selection decision was provided in publicly available World Athletics statements at the time of announcement. Bids are typically assessed on a combination of financial guarantees, government support, venue quality, experience, and strategic objectives — the last of which, in this case, clearly included geographic expansion.

**The Munich confirmation**

The simultaneous selection of Munich for 2031 gives World Athletics two consecutive championships outside its traditional heartland — Africa followed by continental Europe. Munich hosted the European Championships in 2022 with considerable success, making it a lower-risk selection for 2031 while Nairobi takes on the more complex organisational challenge of 2029.

World Athletics' hosting bidding calendar had scheduled the 2029 host selection for September 2026, meaning the Nairobi decision landed on schedule.

The next major dates for the Nairobi 2029 preparation will involve the formal host city agreement and the publication of the event schedule. Coverage will continue as those details emerge.
