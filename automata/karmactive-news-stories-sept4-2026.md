Detroit does have an extensive outdoor warning system — 56 sirens covering seven districts. Those sirens activated during Tuesday's storm, as they should when warnings are issued. That system worked as designed.

Early reports described storms affecting Detroit's east side, and some coverage suggested a tornado touchdown within city limits. Based on the NWS survey released so far, the confirmed touchdown was in Marysville, approximately 40 miles northeast of Detroit.

Anyone in the Detroit area during a tornado warning should immediately move to a basement, an interior room on the lowest floor, or a designated safe room away from windows — consistent with guidance from the City of Detroit's own preparedness resources.
