Older loans linked to the Marginal Cost of Funds-based Lending Rate respond more slowly. Banks recalculate MCLR monthly, but individual accounts reset on their contractual anniversary date. The exact reset frequency is governed by the loan contract.

For fixed deposit savers, a repo rate hike typically signals scope for banks to revise deposit rates, though the timing and quantum depend on each bank's decision. Shifting 8.33% of eligible wages monthly into the pooled, formula-based EPS-95 fund provides different retirement returns than the compounding EPF account depending on tenure and individual circumstances. The EPS-95 fund functions as a defined benefit scheme. The Uttarakhand Board of School Education has released provisional answer keys for UTET Paper I and Paper II following the examination held on September 29, 2026. Candidates have until October 15 to file a challenge on any disputed answer.

Both answer keys are available at `ubse.uk.gov.in` and at the dedicated UTET portal `ukutet.com`. The objection tracker opened on October 5 and closes at 11:59 PM on October 15, 2026. To access the keys, visit either portal, navigate to the UTET documents section, and download the PDF for your paper. For linked state recruitment updates, see [state employment notices for September 2026](https://www.karmactive.com/september-2026-jobs-report-29000-jobs/).

Candidates who took Paper I or Paper II on September 29 should match their responses against the provisional key before the objection window closes. Standard TET qualifying thresholds typically require 60% for general category candidates and 50% for SC, ST, and OBC candidates, but candidates should verify the exact thresholds from the official UTET 2026 notification.

To challenge a question, log into the objection tracker on `ukutet.com`, select the relevant paper, and follow the current notice's directions for submitting an objection and attaching supporting material. The official notice will specify the applicable fee, required evidence format, and submission procedure.

**What is the last date to raise objections on the UTET 2026 answer key?**
The official UTET portal closes the objection tracker at 11:59 PM on October 15, 2026. Log in to `ukutet.com` and submit your challenge with documentary proof before that deadline.

Keep your proof of challenge submission. Final answer keys and UTET 2026 qualifying results will be announced in due course—check `ubse.uk.gov.in` for the official schedule.
