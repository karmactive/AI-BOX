## Article 2: Gonorrhoea and Syphilis Rise More Than 50% in Australia: Who Is Most at Risk?

Australia's rates of gonorrhoea and syphilis have [risen by more than 50% over the past decade](https://www.kirby.unsw.edu.au/news/sti-rates-remain-high-australias-latest-sexual-health-data#:~:text=risen%20by%20more%20than%2050%25), according to new data released by the [Kirby Institute at UNSW](https://www.kirby.unsw.edu.au/news/sti-rates-remain-high-australias-latest-sexual-health-data). The numbers are the largest, most detailed picture of sexual health in the country — and they show a story more complicated than a single national trend.

### The Numbers

In 2025, Australia recorded:

- **[5,986 syphilis diagnoses](https://www.kirby.unsw.edu.au/news/sti-rates-remain-high-australias-latest-sexual-health-data#:~:text=5%2C986%20syphilis)**
- **[42,393 gonorrhoea diagnoses](https://www.kirby.unsw.edu.au/news/sti-rates-remain-high-australias-latest-sexual-health-data#:~:text=42%2C393%20gonorrhoea)**
- **[94,184 chlamydia diagnoses](https://www.kirby.unsw.edu.au/news/sti-rates-remain-high-australias-latest-sexual-health-data#:~:text=94%2C184%20chlamydia)**

Compared to a decade ago, both syphilis and gonorrhoea diagnosis rates have risen by more than 50%. These are notifications — confirmed cases reported to health authorities — not estimates.

Men account for the large majority of diagnoses: 78% of syphilis cases and 73% of gonorrhoea cases. That reflects, in part, that gay and bisexual men remain the population group with the highest testing rates and the highest notification numbers.

### What This Means for You

If you are an Australian adult who is [sexually active](https://www.racgp.org.au/clinical-resources/clinical-guidelines/key-racgp-guidelines/national-guide/sexually-transmissible-infections-and-blood-borne#:~:text=sexually%20active) and not regularly tested, these numbers are relevant to you personally — not as a news item, but as a health prompt.

[Syphilis and gonorrhoea](https://www.racgp.org.au/clinical-resources/clinical-guidelines/key-racgp-guidelines/national-guide/sexually-transmissible-infections-and-blood-borne#:~:text=Syphilis%20and%20gonorrhoea) can be asymptomatic for extended periods. A person can carry and transmit either infection without knowing. Both are curable with antibiotics when caught early; both can cause serious complications when left untreated. Syphilis in pregnancy is particularly serious — it can be passed to an unborn baby, causing congenital syphilis, which can lead to stillbirth or severe illness in a newborn.

The practical message from the data is straightforward: regular STI testing is the only reliable way to know your status.

### Who Is Most Affected

#### Women and pregnancy

The Kirby Institute data shows a concerning rise in syphilis among women of reproductive age. In 2025, [43% of congenital syphilis cases were diagnosed in First Nations infants](https://www.kirby.unsw.edu.au/news/sti-rates-remain-high-australias-latest-sexual-health-data#:~:text=43%25%20of%20congenital%20syphilis) — a figure that reflects both the disparity in testing access and the ongoing challenges of antenatal care in some communities.

Congenital syphilis is preventable. It requires detecting the infection in a pregnant person early enough to treat it before transmission to the baby. The data is a direct argument for routine syphilis testing during pregnancy, particularly in communities where access to antenatal care is inconsistent.

#### First Nations communities

The disparity in STI rates between First Nations and non-Indigenous Australians is a persistent feature of the data. Experts have called for more comprehensive testing in First Nations communities and more accessible pathways to treatment.

#### Gay and bisexual men

This group has historically had the highest STI notification rates, partly because they are also the most tested. But the Kirby Institute data contains a more positive signal here: recent declines in [infectious syphilis and chlamydia](https://www.kirby.unsw.edu.au/news/increases-syphilis-and-gonorrhoea-chlamydia-stable-and-hiv-decline#:~:text=infectious%20syphilis%20and%20chlamydia) among gay and bisexual men suggest that targeted prevention measures — including doxy-PEP, a post-exposure antibiotic treatment — may be having an effect.

That matters for how the national story is read. The 50% decade-long increase is real. But within that, some groups are seeing recent improvement while others are seeing continued deterioration.

### The Counter-Trend That Changes the Story

The headline figure of 50% over a decade is accurate, but it flattens a more textured picture. Among gay and bisexual men — who represent the largest share of historical diagnoses — [syphilis and chlamydia rates have recently declined](https://www.kirby.unsw.edu.au/news/increases-syphilis-and-gonorrhoea-chlamydia-stable-and-hiv-decline#:~:text=syphilis%20and%20chlamydia%20rates%20have%20recently%20declined). That is not a small footnote. It suggests that when prevention tools reach a population group effectively, they work.

The challenge is extending that reach. The groups currently experiencing rising rates — women, First Nations communities, younger Australians — are the ones where testing and prevention resources need to be directed next.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**What is causing the rise in syphilis and gonorrhoea in Australia?**
The Kirby Institute attributes the longer-term rise to a combination of factors including expanded screening, increased awareness, and genuine increases in transmission in some populations. Among gay and bisexual men, recent declines in syphilis and chlamydia are encouraging and may reflect the uptake of prevention measures including doxy-PEP. Overall national rates, however, remain high.

**What is congenital syphilis and why does it matter?**
Congenital syphilis occurs when syphilis is transmitted from a pregnant person to their baby during pregnancy or childbirth. It can cause stillbirth, premature birth, and serious illness in newborns. It is preventable through testing and treatment during pregnancy. In 2025, 43% of congenital syphilis cases in Australia were in First Nations infants.

**Should I get tested for STIs if I have no symptoms?**
Yes. Syphilis and gonorrhoea are commonly asymptomatic — a person can carry and transmit them without knowing. Regular testing is the only reliable way to detect infections early, when they are straightforward to treat with antibiotics.

### What Comes Next

The Kirby Institute releases its sexual health surveillance data annually. The next round will show whether recent declines in some population groups continue, and whether the overall national trend begins to stabilise. Australian health authorities have called for increased testing and prevention resources, particularly in communities where access remains limited.

## Article 3: Australian Off Road Enters Administration After 26 Years: What Customers Need to Know

If you have paid a deposit for an Australian Off Road caravan, have a vehicle currently at the factory, or own an AOR caravan and rely on the company for warranty or service, this article is about your situation. The Queensland-based manufacturer has entered [voluntary administration](https://asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/insolvency/#:~:text=voluntary%20administration) after more than 26 years in business.

### What Happened

[Australian Off Road](https://abr.business.gov.au/ABN/View/97111822283#:~:text=Australian%20Off%20Road), based in Caloundra on Queensland's Sunshine Coast, has entered voluntary administration. The company had been operating for more than 26 years and employed approximately 100 people.

The collapse unfolded quickly and with little warning. Phones were disconnected. The website disappeared. The factory shut. Customers who had appointments to collect finished vehicles arrived and found the gates locked, their caravans visible inside but inaccessible.

Administration was confirmed after ABC News reported the sudden shutdown. The entity behind Australian Off Road is [Rhost Pty Ltd](https://abr.business.gov.au/ABN/View/97111822283#:~:text=Rhost%20Pty%20Ltd), which operates under that ABN.

The company had faced increasing competition from cheaper imported caravans — a pressure that has been building across Australian off-road caravan manufacturing for several years.

### What Customers Are Now Facing

The situation for AOR customers depends on where they sit in the process:

**Customers with deposits on unbuilt or partially built caravans** are [unsecured creditors](https://asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/insolvency/#:~:text=unsecured%20creditors) in the administration process. Their deposits are at risk and will be subject to the administrator's findings and any resulting distribution to creditors.

**Customers with completed caravans at the factory** are in a more immediate position — their vehicles are physically present but cannot be retrieved until the administrator determines what happens to factory assets and outstanding orders.

**Existing AOR owners seeking warranty or service** will need to find alternative service providers. The administration means the manufacturer's warranty obligations cannot be assumed to continue.

### Why Australian Off Road Collapsed

The company faced a combination of market and structural pressures. Australian-made off-road caravans carry a significant price premium over imported alternatives. As imported caravans improved in quality and continued to reduce in price, the cost difference became harder to justify for a portion of the buyer market.

That dynamic has affected multiple Australian manufacturers. Australian Off Road is not the first domestic caravan maker to encounter this pressure, and the industry more broadly is navigating a difficult period as the post-pandemic caravan boom normalises.

### What Administration Means

Voluntary administration is not the same as liquidation. An administrator is appointed to assess the company's financial position and explore all available options — which may include a restructure, a sale of the business as a going concern, or ultimately liquidation if no viable path forward exists.

The administrator takes control of the business during this process. All decisions about existing orders, deposits, vehicles at the factory, and creditor claims are now made through that process, not through the company's management.

Customers should not attempt to retrieve vehicles from the factory without the administrator's explicit authorisation to do so.

### What Customers Should Do Now

1. **Preserve all documentation.** Gather and keep every piece of paper or digital record relating to your AOR purchase: invoice, deposit receipt, purchase contract, correspondence, warranty documentation, and any written communications from the company.

2. **Wait for official contact from the administrator.** The appointed administrator will notify known creditors and customers about the process, timelines, and next steps. Do not act on secondary information until you have received official communication.

3. **[Register as a creditor](https://asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/insolvency/#:~:text=Register%20as%20a%20creditor).** Once the administrator's details are publicly available, customers with financial claims against the company should register as creditors. This is how deposits and outstanding obligations are formally pursued in an administration.

4. **Seek independent legal or financial advice** if you are unsure about your specific situation, particularly regarding significant sums or complex purchase arrangements.

### What Happens to Australian Off Road Customers After Administration?

The outcome for individual customers depends on the administration process. Customers with deposits or outstanding orders are unsecured creditors. Their claims will be assessed alongside other creditors during the administration. Customers should retain all invoices, contracts, deposit records, warranty documents, and correspondence, and register as creditors once the administrator's process is formally established.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I get my caravan from the factory during administration?**
Not without authorisation from the administrator. Even if your caravan is finished and visible at the factory, access to company assets is controlled by the administrator while the process is underway. Contact the appointed administrator directly before taking any steps to retrieve a vehicle.

**Is my AOR warranty still valid?**
The manufacturer's ability to honour warranties is effectively suspended during administration. Existing owners should seek alternative service providers for any current or upcoming maintenance. Whether warranty claims can be pursued as part of the administration is a question for the administrator or a legal adviser.

**What is the difference between administration and liquidation?**
Administration is an assessment period where an independent administrator examines options including restructure, sale, or winding up. It does not automatically mean the company will be liquidated. Liquidation — the final winding up and sale of assets to pay creditors — is one possible outcome, but not the only one.

### What Comes Next

The administrator's assessment will determine whether Australian Off Road continues in some form, is sold, or is wound up. As that process develops, customers will receive formal communication about their claims and next steps. The outcome is not yet determined.

### Why Layoffs Can Happen During Record Growth

This is the part of the story that most news coverage misses.

Growing revenue creates demand for more products and infrastructure. But if those products and that infrastructure can be built and maintained with a smaller team, the workforce requirement does not scale proportionally with revenue.

Oracle's situation is a practical, documented example of that dynamic. Its filings describe restructuring to improve operating efficiency through AI integration. Its results show the strategy is producing revenue growth. Its employees are experiencing the consequence of that efficiency gain.

The Oracle layoffs are not an isolated event. They are part of a broader pattern across large technology companies in which significant AI capital investment is being accompanied by workforce reduction in the same period.

For tech workers — particularly in software engineering, testing, and operational roles — Oracle's own language in its public filings is worth reading directly. It describes AI code generation as enabling the company to build more with fewer people. That is not a future risk. It is a current restructuring rationale from one of the world's largest enterprise software companies.

## Article 9: UPI Charges Above ₹2,000: Will You Have to Pay a Fee for ₹5,000 Payments?

If you regularly make [UPI payments](https://www.npci.org.in/#:~:text=UPI%20payments) above ₹2,000 — whether you are paying rent, splitting a bill, or sending money to family — the short answer is: **no, you will not automatically be charged a fee on your regular bank-to-bank UPI payment because of this notification.**

Here is what the government actually notified, what it means, and where the real question lies.

### What the Government Actually Notified

The Government of India issued a [Gazette notification on September 14](https://egazette.gov.in/#:~:text=Gazette%20notification%20September%2014) that specifically protects [UPI transactions up to ₹2,000](https://egazette.gov.in/#:~:text=UPI%20transactions%20up%20to%20%E2%82%B92%2C000) from charges by banks and payment system providers.

Read that again: the notification protects payments **up to** ₹2,000. It does not, on its own, create a new fee schedule for payments **above** ₹2,000.

The ₹2,000 figure is a threshold for a no-charge protection — not a new fee trigger.

### What MDR Is and Why It Matters

MDR stands for Merchant Discount Rate. It is a fee that can be charged on certain merchant transactions — payments made at a shop, on an app, or through a platform where a business receives money.

The current confusion stems from a broader policy question: whether an [MDR framework](https://www.npci.org.in/#:~:text=MDR%20framework) for higher-value UPI transactions involving prepaid payment instruments (PPIs) — such as digital wallets and prepaid cards — might be introduced.

For ordinary [bank-to-bank UPI payments](https://www.npci.org.in/#:~:text=bank-to-bank%20UPI%20payments), the no-MDR framework has been in place and is reinforced by this notification for transactions up to ₹2,000. The notification does not automatically impose charges on payments above that threshold.

The policy question around what happens above ₹2,000 — particularly for merchant transactions using PPIs — remains the subject of ongoing discussion between NPCI, banks, and the government.

### P2P vs Merchant Transactions: A Critical Distinction

Not all UPI payments are the same.

**[P2P payments](https://www.npci.org.in/#:~:text=P2P%20payments)** — when you send money directly from your bank account to another person's bank account via UPI — operate differently from **merchant payments**. Ordinary peer-to-peer transfers between bank accounts are not the primary focus of MDR discussions.

**Merchant payments** — when a business receives money through a UPI-linked system, particularly one involving a PPI — are where MDR discussions are most relevant. If you pay for something at a store or app using a UPI-linked wallet or prepaid card, the merchant's transaction may be subject to different rules than a bank-to-bank transfer.

The practical upshot: if you are sending ₹5,000 or ₹10,000 directly from your bank account to a family member or a friend via standard UPI, this notification does not create a fee on that transaction.

### What Consumers Should Expect

Right now:
- Ordinary bank-account UPI payments are not newly subject to consumer-side charges as a result of this notification
- The ₹2,000 no-charge protection is reinforced and confirmed
- What happens with MDR for higher-value merchant transactions above ₹2,000 depends on further policy decisions that have not yet been formally announced for standard consumer payments

The [Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-economics/upi-charges-above-2000-mdr-consumers-explained-10878576/#:~:text=Indian%20Express) notes the distinction between ordinary bank-account UPI (where free transfers remain the baseline) and PPI-linked transactions where the MDR question is more active.

Rahul Gandhi and the opposition have raised political concerns about whether this notification effectively ["opened the door"](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/quietly-opened-the-door-to-imposing-fees-on-upi-rahul-gandhi-attacks-centre-12048562#:~:text=opened%20the%20door) to future charges. The government's position is that UPI remains free for ordinary users. That policy debate is ongoing.

The responsible reading of the September 14 notification is this: it protects payments up to ₹2,000 from charges. It does not confirm that payments above ₹2,000 will or will not be charged in future — that remains to be determined by further official decisions.

### Will UPI Payments Above ₹2,000 Be Charged?

The [September 14 government notification](https://egazette.gov.in/#:~:text=September%2014%20government%20notification) specifically prevents charges on UPI transactions **up to** ₹2,000. It does not itself impose a consumer fee on payments above ₹2,000. For ordinary bank-account UPI transfers, free payments remain the baseline. The broader question of MDR for higher-value merchant transactions involving PPIs is subject to further policy development — no formal announcement of consumer-side charges above ₹2,000 has been confirmed.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Will my ₹5,000 bank-to-bank UPI payment cost extra now?**
For a standard transfer directly between two bank accounts via UPI, the September 14 notification does not create a new fee. The ₹2,000 no-charge protection is confirmed; what happens above that threshold depends on further government and NPCI decisions that have not been finalised for ordinary bank-account transfers.

**What is the ₹2,000 UPI notification actually saying?**
The [Government of India's September 14 Gazette notification](https://egazette.gov.in/#:~:text=Government%20of%20India%27s%20September%2014%20Gazette%20notification) states that no bank or payment system provider may impose charges on specified electronic payment modes — including UPI — for transactions up to ₹2,000. It protects lower-value payments from being charged. It does not itself introduce fees on higher-value payments.

**What is MDR and does it apply to my UPI payments?**
[MDR (Merchant Discount Rate)](https://www.npci.org.in/#:~:text=MDR%20%28Merchant%20Discount%20Rate%29) is a fee that can apply to certain merchant transactions. It is most relevant to payments made through prepaid payment instruments (PPIs) like digital wallets and prepaid cards, not to ordinary bank-to-bank UPI transfers. Whether MDR will apply to higher-value UPI merchant transactions is an open policy question, not a confirmed outcome.

### What Comes Next

NPCI and the government are expected to clarify the MDR framework for higher-value transactions further. The political debate about UPI charges is ongoing. As implementation details are confirmed, the practical rules for consumers will become clearer. Monitor announcements from [NPCI](https://www.npci.org.in/#:~:text=NPCI) and the Ministry of Finance for official guidance.
