# Rockstar Games Tribunal Hears Discord Emails Were an "Egregious" Policy Breach

---

## ARTICLE

If you follow GTA VI, you probably know Rockstar North dismissed a large group of employees earlier this year. What is now playing out in a UK employment tribunal is the dispute over whether those dismissals were legal — and a Discord server is at the centre of both sides' case.

### The Rockstar Discord Tribunal: Key Facts

A UK employment tribunal is hearing claims from former Rockstar North employees who say they were unfairly dismissed in connection with trade-union activity. Thirty-four people are believed to have been dismissed. Twenty-three are participating in the Rockstar Games Discord tribunal.

Rockstar disputes the union-activity claim. The company says the dismissals followed gross misconduct — specifically, that employees shared confidential company emails through a Discord server in what Rockstar describes as an "egregious" breach of policy.

The tribunal is examining both sets of claims. It has not issued a judgment.

### What the Discord Server Means to Both Sides

The tribunal is relevant to GTA VI readers because the dispute involves former Rockstar North employees who worked on the game, but the proceedings have not established that Rockstar unlawfully dismissed them. Former workers say the dismissals were linked to union activity, while Rockstar says they followed gross misconduct involving confidential information. The distinction should remain explicit.

The Discord server is not an incidental detail. It sits at the centre of Rockstar's case and the workers' case simultaneously, for different reasons.

Rockstar's argument is that employees used the server to share confidential company emails — the "egregious" breach it has presented to the tribunal. The former employees' account is that the server was used for workplace organisation, which is a protected activity under UK labour law when it relates to trade union activity. Whether the server's primary function was protected workplace organisation or policy-breaching information sharing is central to what the tribunal must decide.

### What Each Side Claims

Former employees allege that Rockstar dismissed them because of their union activity, not because of their conduct. Under UK employment law, dismissal for trade union activity is automatically unfair dismissal. That is a significant legal protection — if established, it removes the employer's ability to justify the terminations.

Rockstar's position is that the dismissals were for gross misconduct related to confidential information shared through Discord. That is a separate legal basis which, if established by evidence, can be a valid reason for dismissal.

The tribunal has heard evidence from both sides. Nothing in the proceedings so far constitutes a finding that either version is correct. The tribunal is still examining the competing claims.

### Why GTA VI Makes This a Bigger Story

GTA VI is scheduled for release on November 19, 2026. Some of the workers involved in the tribunal worked on the game during their time at Rockstar North in Glasgow. The game's release date means this dispute is unlikely to drop from public attention before launch.

The timing creates an unusual backdrop: a major game release and an active employment tribunal involving people who contributed to it, running simultaneously. The tribunal itself does not concern the game's development or quality — but the audience overlap between GTA VI followers and people tracking the Rockstar North employment case is significant.

### The Broader Significance

The case matters beyond the immediate parties. How UK employment tribunals treat digital communication tools — Discord, Slack, internal messaging platforms — as evidence in dismissal cases is a developing area of law. The argument that a Discord server used for workplace discussion constitutes grounds for gross misconduct, versus the argument that the same server represents protected union organising, touches questions that affect employers and employees across the technology and games industries.
