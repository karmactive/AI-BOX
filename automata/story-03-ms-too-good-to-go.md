# M&S Too Good To Go Bags: Prices, Stores, and How to Secure Surplus Bakery Treats

---

## ARTICLE

M&S has launched surplus bakery bags through the Too Good To Go app at more than 350 stores across the UK and Ireland, offering shoppers a guaranteed minimum of £10.20 worth of fresh bakery goods for **£3.80** — roughly one-third of their shelf value. The partnership, confirmed in an official M&S press release on September 14, 2026, covers 354 store locations and is focused exclusively on in-store bakery surplus.

You can now secure £10 to £12 worth of fresh M&S bakery goods for £3.80 through the Too Good To Go app at over 350 locations nationwide. To secure a bag, open the app in the late afternoon, find your nearest participating M&S Food store, reserve your bag digitally, and collect it from customer services during the designated evening window before store closing. Bags sell out quickly — checking the app between 4pm and 6pm gives the best chance of finding available stock.

### What Is Inside an M&S Too Good To Go Bag

The bags contain surplus in-store bakery items nearing their sell-by date. M&S has confirmed these are bakery products only — artisan loaves, rolls, croissants, pastries, and similar items from the in-store bakery section. The contents vary by location and by the surplus available on any given day, which is the nature of the Too Good To Go model: you reserve a "surprise bag" without selecting specific products.

The initiative does **not** include chilled ready meals, prepared salads, or other fresh food categories. It is strictly a bakery programme, which is an important clarification for shoppers hoping to apply it more broadly.

The guaranteed minimum value is £10.20 per bag, at a price of £3.80, giving a value ratio of roughly 2.7 to 1. In practice, many bags exceed the minimum value depending on what surplus is available.

### How the Too Good To Go Booking Works

Unlike standing in the supermarket aisle hunting for yellow-sticker items before closing time, the Too Good To Go system shifts surplus clearance into a digital reservation market. Shoppers reserve a bag through the app — typically during the late afternoon — and the reservation locks the stock before it reaches the physical reduced shelf.

This matters because M&S bakery items are not typically discounted through the in-store yellow sticker system until very late in the trading day. The app reservation window gives budget shoppers access to M&S quality at a price point that was previously unavailable through normal shopping.

To use the service:

1. Download or open the Too Good To Go app
2. Search for M&S Food near your location
3. Select a bag when one becomes available — stock often appears from mid-afternoon
4. Pay in the app (£3.80) and receive a collection window
5. Collect from the M&S store customer services desk during the designated time slot

Setting up push notifications for your local store in the app settings will alert you the moment bags go live, which is the most reliable way to secure one before they sell out.

### The Food Waste Context

M&S has redistributed more than **57,000 tonnes of surplus food** to date, equivalent to more than 130 million meals donated to charitable partners through its existing Neighbourly food share network. The Too Good To Go partnership adds a direct consumer channel to that infrastructure.

M&S has set a goal of halving its food waste by 2030. The Too Good To Go rollout addresses the bakery category specifically because it represents high daily surplus volume — fresh bread and pastries baked in store that cannot be sold the following day.

The programme complements existing charity food sharing rather than replacing it. Surplus that is not reserved through the app by closing time continues to be donated through Neighbourly before disposal.
