# Prince Harry's Children Change School After Just Two Days Over Security Concerns

Prince Harry and Meghan's children, Archie and Lilibet, have left their new UK school after just two days. The reason was not an incident at the school — it was a logistics problem. The family's security team raised concerns about the practicalities of getting the children there safely, and a different school has been arranged.

## What Happened

Archie and Lilibet recently started at a school in the UK following the Sussex family's return to Britain. Within two days, it became clear the arrangements weren't workable.

The family's spokesperson confirmed the move and was direct about the reason: the security team had concerns about the distance, heavy traffic conditions, and the overall transportation arrangements between the family's home and the school. The statement also said the decision should not be interpreted as any criticism of the school, its staff, or its community.

The children are expected to begin at a different school shortly.

## What This Does and Doesn't Mean

This is a logistics adjustment, not a security emergency. There has been no reported threat against the school or the children. The concern, as described by the family's own statement, was practical — getting two young children safely to and from school in conditions the security team considered unworkable.

That distinction matters. Coverage framing this as a "security scare" overstates what has been confirmed. The family's spokesperson used the words "practicalities of the arrangements" — which is a very different thing from a reported threat.

For parents following this story, the most relevant takeaway is simple: the children are changing schools for the same kind of logistical reasons that affect any family's school choice — proximity, traffic, and the daily reality of the commute. The difference is that the Sussex family's commute includes a professional security team.

## The Security Review Context

Harry's security situation in the UK has been a running legal and institutional dispute. He no longer receives automatic police protection as a non-working royal, and has fought publicly and through the courts to have that protection reinstated when he is in the country.

The Royal and VIP Executive Committee — known as RAVEC — is the body that oversees protective security decisions for the royal family and associated individuals. A review of Harry and Meghan's security arrangements is currently underway following their return to the UK.

That review is separate from the school decision. The school move is about transportation logistics under existing arrangements, not about the outcome of the RAVEC assessment.

Meghan is also reported to be undergoing a separate security review.

## Why Did Harry and Meghan's Children Change School?

Prince Harry and Meghan moved Archie and Lilibet to a different school after their security team raised concerns about the practicalities of transporting the children between their home and the original school. The specific factors cited were distance and heavy traffic. The family stated clearly that the decision was not a criticism of the original school or its staff.

## What Happens Next

The children will attend a different school. The RAVEC security review for Harry and Meghan is ongoing and its outcome will be a separate development. When that assessment concludes, it will determine the longer-term protective arrangements for the family during their time in the UK.
