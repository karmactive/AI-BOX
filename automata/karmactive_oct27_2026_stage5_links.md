## STORY 1 — Anthropic Files S-1 for Nasdaq IPO Under Ticker ANT

**Anthropic Files S-1 for Nasdaq IPO Under Ticker ANT: $16.3 Billion Revenue, $80.7 Billion Loss — Explained**

[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com), the company behind the Claude AI assistant, officially filed paperwork with U.S. regulators on October 26 to go public on the [Nasdaq Global Select Market](https://www.nasdaq.com/) under the ticker symbol "ANT." The reaction came fast, and most of it fixated on the same startling number: an $80.7 billion net loss.

That number is almost entirely misleading, and here is why it matters to understand that before anyone touches this stock.

The company's real operating loss for the first half of 2026 was $693 million — an accrual-accounting figure reflecting costs including staff, computing power, and infrastructure — set against $16.3 billion in revenue over those same six months. That works out to roughly four cents per dollar of operating loss against every dollar Anthropic earned. The $80.7 billion headline figure includes a $72.4 billion non-cash accounting adjustment. Early investors in Anthropic received convertible notes before the company went public. As Anthropic's estimated worth climbed toward $1.5 trillion to $2 trillion, accounting standards required the company to record the rising value of those notes as a loss on its financial statements. No cash went anywhere.

Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are managing the offering. Investors in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia are named in the filing as eligible markets. The registration statement is not yet effective; no securities can be sold until the SEC formally declares it so.

The share structure inside the filing is the part most coverage skipped entirely. Anthropic created multiple classes of stock that separate economic ownership from actual control of the company.

Public buyers on the Nasdaq will receive Class A shares, which give economic exposure to the company's performance — your shares may rise or fall in value; revenue is not automatically distributed to shareholders. The filing also creates Class N shares, which certain institutional investors including [Amazon Web Services](https://aws.amazon.com/) receive through convertible note conversions. A single Class F share, held by a Founder LLC on behalf of the seven co-founders, carries 50.1 percent of total voting power, meaning the founders retain governance control over the company regardless of how many Class A shares are sold to the public. Class T directors sit at the governance level that drives the company's direction. In plain terms, retail investors buying ANT on listing day will own a stake in a fast-growing AI business, but the people running Anthropic and setting its mission are structurally insulated from public shareholder pressure. Multi-class share structures are common in tech IPOs, but Anthropic's version is more layered than most.

Infrastructure spending is another major theme inside the filing. Training frontier AI models at the scale Anthropic operates requires data centers consuming enormous quantities of electricity. The S-1 lists power grid capacity as a direct business risk. Capital expenditure on compute and energy infrastructure is projected to remain heavy for years, which is one reason operating losses are likely to continue even as revenue climbs steeply.

Revenue growth has been extraordinary by any measure. The $16.3 billion for the first half of 2026 came primarily from enterprise API access to Claude and from cloud partnerships with Amazon Web Services and Google Cloud. Businesses are embedding Claude-powered features into their own products at an accelerating pace.

The filing also extends to investors in the European Economic Area, Japan, and Switzerland through separate country-specific provisions, without requiring Anthropic to list on local exchanges in those markets.

The next document to watch is an amended filing called the S-1/A, which will disclose the actual price range per share and the total number of shares being offered. That is expected sometime in November 2026, with a roadshow potentially beginning around the week of November 9, though neither date is confirmed. Trading cannot begin until the registration statement is declared effective and the offering is priced.

For anyone who will never buy a single ANT share, this [IPO](https://karmactive.com/tag/ipo/) still matters. The price Anthropic's stock opens at will become the first publicly traded benchmark for the value of a frontier AI lab. Every private AI company — OpenAI, xAI, and others — will be measured against that number for years.

## STORY 2 — AT&T Data Breach Settlement Payout Timeline

**AT&T Data Breach Settlement Payout Timeline: The November Dates That Decide When Your Check Arrives**

If you filed a claim in the AT&T data breach settlement, the date that matters right now is Monday, November 2, 2026 — not December, not whenever checks are supposedly going out. Here is the legal chain that explains why.

AT&T agreed to a $177 million class-action settlement covering two separate data breaches: one in March 2024 and one in July 2024. A federal judge in the [U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Texas](https://www.txnd.uscourts.gov/) formally approved the deal on October 2, 2026. That date started a legal clock that controls everything else on the payout timeline.

Under federal court procedure, any objector has 30 days from the final approval order to file a notice of appeal. That 30-day window closes on Monday, November 2, 2026. If that deadline passes without an appeal, the settlement enters what the agreement defines as its "Effective Date" — which arrives 10 days later, on November 12, 2026. From that point, the settlement administrator has 60 days to issue payments. Run that math and the first wave of checks or digital transfers is expected to arrive between mid-December 2026 and mid-January 2027, though no exact payment date has been formally announced.

The catch: if even a single objector files a notice of appeal before November 2, the Effective Date and all subsequent payment deadlines are suspended until the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit resolves the matter, a process that can take many months or more.

The $5,000 figure circulating in most headlines is also worth correcting before anyone gets the wrong expectation. That cap applies exclusively to claimants who submitted documented proof of out-of-pocket financial harm — verified fraud losses, identity theft costs, or similar expenses — before the December 2025 claims deadline. Standard claimants without documentation receive a much smaller share: the settlement administrator estimates roughly $39 to $40 for Tier 1 claimants (those whose Social Security numbers were exposed), with smaller amounts for lower tiers.

The claims filing window closed in December 2025. There is no option to file a new claim at this stage.

For those who did file on time: verify that your payment details on file with the settlement administrator are current before November 12. Do this only through the official settlement website to avoid fraudulent communications. If your bank account details or mailing address have changed since you submitted your claim, updating them through the official channel before the Effective Date is the best way to receive your payment in the first distribution wave.

The two 2024 [data breaches](https://karmactive.com/tag/security/) together exposed tens of millions of AT&T customers' personal data. The settlement covers anyone whose information was included in either incident.

November 2 is the real checkpoint. If that day passes clean — no appeal filed — then nothing stands between claimants and their payment except the administrator's 60-day processing window. Watch for any filing activity in the Northern District of Texas on Monday. If there is silence, the payout schedule moves forward.

## STORY 3 — Microsoft Offers Voluntary Buyouts to 60,000 Employees

**Microsoft Offers Voluntary Buyouts to 60,000 Employees in US, UK, Canada, and India: Eligibility and Severance Terms**

[Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/) is offering voluntary buyouts to roughly 60,000 employees — more than a quarter of its entire global workforce — in a program that extends further up the management ladder than most people expected.

The details came in an internal memo sent on October 26 by Amy Coleman, Microsoft's Executive Vice President and Chief People Officer. The Voluntary Exit Offer applies to selected teams in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and India.

The headline element is how high the offer reaches. Eligibility goes up to Level 70 on Microsoft's internal grading scale, which includes general managers and some vice presidents below the Corporate VP level. There is no minimum age requirement, no minimum tenure, and no performance rating condition. If you are in an eligible team at the right level, you qualify.

Employees who receive the offer have until the first week of December 2026 to accept or decline. Those who opt in stay on payroll through the end of the year, with exits effective December 31, 2026. Microsoft confirmed only that the package is comparable to its standard severance offering, which varies by market. Reported terms include a minimum of 60 days of base pay and up to 39 weeks depending on seniority and tenure, alongside continued benefits through departure, though Microsoft has not publicly confirmed every term.

Coleman's memo stated: "This is a time-limited, voluntary program that gives those who are eligible the opportunity to leave Microsoft with financial support and career planning resources." She also noted that not all teams within Microsoft are participating — which organizations are offering the program depends on each business unit's priorities, customer commitments, and workforce needs.

The December 31, 2026 exit date is not accidental. That date coincides with the close of Microsoft's second fiscal quarter for FY2027. Exiting 60,000 eligible positions by the end of Q2 FY27 — while retaining those employees through the critical fourth calendar quarter enterprise sales cycle — delivers a clean structural change to headcount. Genuinely voluntary departures under incentive programs generally do not trigger [federal WARN Act](https://www.dol.gov/) notification obligations, though the final determination depends on each organization's specific circumstances and participation structure.

The 60,000 figure represents those who are eligible, not the guaranteed number of departures. How many people actually accept the offer will be known after the early December decision deadline. Microsoft expects a smaller take-up rate than its earlier voluntary retirement program, where approximately 30 percent of eligible employees accepted.

For context: Microsoft employed roughly 223,000 people heading into this program. Earlier in 2026, the company ran a separate retirement-focused buyout aimed at a much narrower group with specific age and tenure criteria. This offer removes those filters entirely and reaches deeper into senior management.

If the voluntary take-up rate falls short of internal workforce reduction targets, watchers of Microsoft's quarterly results will be looking closely at what — if anything — the company announces in Q1 2027. ## STORY 5 — DR Congo Ebola Outbreak Passes 10,000 Cases: Why the Bundibugyo Strain Changes Everything

**DR Congo Ebola Outbreak Passes 10,000 Cases: CDC Risk Update and Why the Bundibugyo Strain Has No Approved Vaccine**

The Democratic Republic of Congo's Ebola outbreak passed a grim threshold on October 27, with the government's public health institute reporting 10,060 confirmed infections and 4,887 deaths. It is the fastest-growing Ebola outbreak ever recorded, and the core reason it spread this quickly is that none of the world's existing approved Ebola vaccines were designed to fight it.

This outbreak is caused by the Bundibugyo virus, a distinct species within the broader Ebola family. The only vaccines in approved stockpiles — including the widely deployed Ervebo shot used effectively in previous DRC outbreaks — were designed specifically for the Zaire ebolavirus species. Against Bundibugyo, they have not been shown to be effective. That gap removed the primary containment tool responders have relied on for the past decade.

The numbers reflect the difference clearly. In 2018, DR Congo's Zaire-strain outbreak took 235 days from response activation to reach 1,000 confirmed cases. The 2026 Bundibugyo outbreak hit the same mark in 40 days. It is now the second-largest Ebola outbreak in history, surpassed only by the West African epidemic of 2014 to 2016, which recorded more than 28,600 cases.

Bundibugyo cases are also harder to identify early. Initial symptoms — fever, fatigue, body aches — closely resemble malaria, which is prevalent across the affected region. Ebola is not typically transmitted during the incubation period before symptoms appear; patients become infectious once they begin showing symptoms, but cases may go undetected for days during that symptomatic window before isolation protocols are triggered.

The outbreak has spread beyond DR Congo's borders. Uganda recorded 20 confirmed cases before being declared Ebola-free in August 2026, and has since reimposed a 21-day mandatory quarantine for travelers returning from the DRC as a precautionary measure. Kenya has now reported at least two deaths and multiple cases, all linked to travel through Uganda, with authorities saying there is no evidence of community spread. In Nigeria, Swiss authorities are investigating a case involving the wife of a Swiss diplomat who was diagnosed there; both she and her husband were transported to Swiss hospitals. Swiss investigators are examining how she contracted the virus, as her travel history does not include the DRC. As of October 26, the [U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention](https://www.cdc.gov/) confirms zero domestic cases in the United States, with the risk of spread to the US remaining very low.

This is DR Congo's 17th recorded Ebola outbreak. The country has built substantial institutional response experience over decades. But the lack of an approved vaccine against this strain has stripped the most effective tool from that experience base.

One development offers longer-term hope. The [World Health Organization](https://www.who.int/) has confirmed that a first clinical trial for a potential Bundibugyo-specific vaccine, called "Tumaini" — meaning "hope" in Swahili — has been launched in Bunia, in the Ituri region. Phase 2/3 trials involve testing safety and effectiveness in a larger human population. This stage typically takes at least a year or more to produce reliable efficacy data, and regulatory approval follows after that. No quick fix is available.

For travelers and aid workers in [East and Central Africa](https://karmactive.com/category/world/africa/): border restrictions are active across the region. Public health authorities recommend avoiding nonessential travel to affected areas in the DRC and following contact tracing guidance if returning from those zones.

## STORY 6 — Israel Votes in First National Election Since October 7 War

**Israel Election 2026: When Exit Polls Close Today, Key Candidates, and the 61-Seat Coalition Math**

Israelis are voting today in the first national Knesset election since the October 7, 2023 attacks. Polling stations across the country opened at 7:00 AM and close at 10:00 PM local time on October 27, 2026. There are 120 seats at stake, and tonight's exit poll numbers — whenever they drop — will not tell you who the next prime minister is.

The main contest runs between Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, leading his Likud party, and a centrist opposition anchored by Gadi Eisenkot. Former prime ministers Naftali Bennett and Yair Lapid have formed a cross-spectrum alliance that gives the anti-Netanyahu bloc a more unified structure than in recent elections. The latest polling averages show Likud and Eisenkot's Yashar party running neck and neck at around 22 seats each.

At 10:00 PM local time — 4:00 PM EDT and 8:00 PM GMT — Israeli television networks will publish their first exit poll projections. These are seat estimates, not certified results. They give a directional read on which bloc is ahead, but they have repeatedly failed to capture the final margin in Israeli elections. The reason is structural.

Israel uses a proportional representation system with a 3.25 percent electoral threshold. Any party that does not clear that threshold does not qualify for [Knesset](https://www.knesset.gov.il/) seats. Votes cast for those parties are excluded from seat allocation, which can indirectly change the number of seats won by parties that do pass the threshold, in ways that are difficult to model accurately until the full count is in.

The Central Elections Committee has registered 38 separate party slates for this election. One of the newer entries is called "The Reservists," identified on the official ballot by the Hebrew letters דּי, formed specifically to represent soldiers and military families carrying the burden of three continuous years of wartime reserve duty. If several small protest parties, including the Reservists, fall just short of the 3.25 percent threshold after all votes are tallied, it can shift the critical 61-seat majority line between the Netanyahu bloc and the opposition.

The main fault lines in this election are layered. The ultra-Orthodox community's longstanding military draft exemption has become a sharp political wound after three years of intense reserve mobilisation. Ongoing questions about the regional conflict's management, the status of ceasefire negotiations, and international sanctions and diplomatic pressure all sit at the centre of the campaign.

Military and diplomatic ballots — cast by soldiers on active duty and staff at Israeli embassies abroad — are counted separately under a "double-envelope" procedure and take additional days to process. That is one reason the full vote count is not expected until October 30. Official certified results from the Central Elections Committee follow on November 4.

After certification, [Israel's president](https://www.gov.il/en) consults with party representatives and formally tasks a Knesset member judged capable of assembling a governing coalition to attempt to form a government. That coalition negotiation process is separate from the vote count itself and can take weeks.

For international readers watching tonight: the first directional signal arrives with exit polls at 4:00 PM EDT and 8:00 PM GMT. Early actual vote tallies begin flowing in over the following hours. A credible picture of which bloc holds the larger share will likely emerge by late evening Eastern time, but the seat count that determines who can form a government will not be confirmed until October 30.

## STORY 7 — Record El Niño 2026–27 Winter Outlook

**Record El Niño 2026–27 Winter Forecast: What NOAA and the WMO Actually Predict for US and UK Weather**

Winter weather headlines have been running hard on warnings of a "polar vortex snow bomb" bearing down on the UK and northern United States. The agencies actually responsible for those forecasts say something quite different.

The underlying driver is real and significant. The El Niño climate pattern — caused by warmer-than-average sea surface temperatures in the central and eastern Pacific Ocean — is building toward potentially record strength. Sea surface temperature anomalies averaged plus 1.7 degrees Celsius above normal between July and September 2026. The [World Meteorological Organization](https://public.wmo.int/en) projects Pacific sea surface temperatures could reach up to 3.8 degrees Celsius above average between November 2026 and January 2027, a level WMO Secretary-General Celeste Saulo says has never appeared in the agency's records — the strongest historical El Niños have reached around 3 degrees Celsius above average. [NOAA's](https://www.noaa.gov/) October 8 Climate Prediction Center update puts an 83 percent probability on this becoming an exceptionally strong El Niño event.

That is genuinely alarming at a global humanitarian level. What it does not mean is that a severe freeze is coming for the northern United States or Britain.

Both NOAA's 2026–27 US Winter Outlook and the [UK Met Office's](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/) seasonal forecast project the opposite pattern in the northern tier. NOAA is calling for warmer-than-average temperatures across the Pacific Northwest, Northern Rockies, and Great Lakes region, with drier conditions there as well. At the same time, the US Southwest, Gulf Coast, and East Coast are forecast to see wetter-than-average conditions — helpful for regions still carrying drought deficits. The NWS also cautions that seasonal outlooks express probabilities over several months and cannot reliably predict individual snowstorms, cold spells, or seasonal snowfall totals months in advance.

The UK Met Office is projecting milder and wetter conditions overall for Britain this winter. The "polar vortex" collapse scenario being promoted in parts of the UK press has no support in official seasonal forecasts.

What is genuinely serious about this El Niño is the effect outside North America and Europe. WMO Secretary-General Celeste Saulo and UN OCHA chief Tom Fletcher have both issued warnings about cascading humanitarian consequences. The African Development Bank estimates this El Niño event could cost African economies between $10 billion and $20 billion — roughly 1 to 2 percent of GDP — and may push approximately 50 million people toward food insecurity as rainfall patterns shift across the continent.

The mechanism is agricultural. El Niño events alter global precipitation distribution. In East Africa, the WMO expects wetter conditions and a heightened flooding risk. In Southern Africa, reduced rainfall cuts maize and sorghum harvests. In South Asia, disruption to the monsoon pattern affects rice and wheat production at scale.

Those disruptions eventually reach food prices. When multiple major agricultural regions face severe growing-season damage simultaneously, global commodity markets for grains, coffee, cocoa, and sugar can see price increases that work their way into retail shelves months later. That is a lagged effect, not an immediate one, but it is the channel through which a Pacific Ocean temperature anomaly eventually becomes visible in grocery shopping in the US and UK.

The bottom line for the coming months: if you are in the northern United States, the official forecast is a milder, drier winter, not a historic freeze. If you are in the US Southwest, Gulf Coast, or East Coast, expect above-average rainfall. If you are in the UK, expect a mild, wet winter. The dangerous version of this El Niño is happening in Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia, where the consequences are already materialising in food and [agricultural markets](https://karmactive.com/category/environment/climate/).

NOAA and the WMO are scheduled to release updated seasonal outlooks in mid-November. Those will be the last major forecasting checkpoints before northern hemisphere winter conditions fully set in.

## STORY 10 — Northwestern Physicists Trap a Single Particle of Antimatter for Months

**Northwestern Physicists Trap a Single Antimatter Positron for Months to Test the Standard Model**

Scientists at [Northwestern University](https://www.northwestern.edu/) have managed to trap a single particle of antimatter and hold it for months without it vanishing. The result was published in [Physical Review Letters](https://journals.aps.org/prl/) on October 21, 2026, and what the team had to do to pull it off is worth understanding.

Antimatter is the mirror version of ordinary matter. A positron is the antimatter counterpart of an electron — same mass, opposite electrical charge. The problem with antimatter is fundamental: the instant it touches ordinary matter, both the positron and the matter particle it hits destroy each other in a burst of energy. Containing a positron long enough to study it with high precision has been the challenge; what this experiment achieves is not simply long storage, but precise quantum control of a trapped positron — something that had not been demonstrated before at this level.

The team, led by physicist Gerald Gabrielse with doctoral student Thomas Myers as first author, worked with radioactive sodium-22, which naturally emits positrons as it decays. They captured a released positron, then slowed and cooled it to approximately 300 millikelvins — 0.3 degrees above absolute zero, the coldest temperature that physically exists. At that near-zero temperature, the positron was confined by precisely shaped electric and magnetic fields in a device the team calls a one-positron quantum cyclotron. The positron never touches any physical surface, which prevents it from annihilating.

The key experimental result is that the team achieved precise quantum control: the positron's energy can only change in fixed, discrete steps, and those steps can be triggered and observed one by one. This level of control was not previously possible with a single trapped positron.

The reason for all this effort is to measure one specific property with unprecedented precision: how strongly a positron responds to a magnetic field. This property is called the magnetic moment. The Standard Model of particle physics — the current best framework for describing how matter and energy work at the subatomic level — predicts that an electron and a positron should respond identically to the same magnetic field, just with opposite signs.

If a future measurement reveals even a tiny deviation from that prediction, it would be evidence that the Standard Model is incomplete. That kind of deviation would point toward undiscovered particles, undiscovered forces, or both. Physicists have been searching for such deviations for decades because the Standard Model, despite its extraordinary predictive success, is known to be incomplete — it cannot account for dark matter, for instance, or explain the imbalance between matter and antimatter in the observable universe.

The trapping and quantum control capability the Northwestern team has now demonstrated establishes the platform for that future high-precision comparison. The measurement itself — the direct comparison of the positron and electron magnetic moments to test where the Standard Model breaks down — is the goal this experiment now makes achievable. **Tsunami Warning Lifted After Magnitude 6.9 Earthquake and 6.3 Aftershock Strike Off Vanuatu**

A magnitude 6.9 earthquake struck off the coast of Vanuatu on Saturday, October 24, triggering coastal wave advisories that were subsequently lifted after monitoring agencies reassessed the threat.

The earthquake occurred 197 kilometers west-northwest of Port Olry, at a shallow depth of 10 kilometers. The [Pacific Tsunami Warning Center](https://ptwc.weather.gov/) initially assessed the larger event at magnitude 7.2 before the [U.S. Geological Survey](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/) issued a revised magnitude of 6.9 following additional seismic analysis. A magnitude 6.3 aftershock followed within minutes, and at least nine earthquakes ranging from 4.5 to 6.9 in magnitude have since been recorded in the region.

The Pacific Tsunami Warning Center first warned of possible hazardous waves within 300 kilometers of the epicenter. After reassessing sea-level observations and seismic data, authorities lifted all advisories. Vanuatu's Geological Hazards Department confirmed there is no longer a tsunami threat to coastal communities. Australia's Bureau of Meteorology reported that no threat was assessed for the Australian coast.

No injuries and no structural damage have been reported. The immediate threat has passed.

Vanuatu sits within the Pacific Ring of Fire, an arc of seismic and volcanic activity stretching across the Pacific basin. Magnitude 6 to 7 earthquakes in the region occur regularly. The shallow depth of this event — 10 kilometers — raises the potential for tsunami generation in seismic events, which is why monitoring systems activated immediately. The decision to lift the warning was based on observed sea-level measurements across monitoring networks, not on magnitude alone.

Aftershocks remain a normal pattern after an [earthquake](https://karmactive.com/tag/earthquake/) of this size and are possible in the days following the main event. Residents in coastal areas of northern Vanuatu were advised to monitor updates from local emergency management authorities.
