## Critical Issues (Flagged by Both Models)

### 1. Mathematical Error: 340% ≠ 45,000 Reports
**Articles:** 1, 3  
**Claim:** "340% increase from 2021 = 45,000 reports"  
**Math Check:** 8,600 × 3.4 = 29,240 ≠ 45,000  
**Status:** ❌ DISPUTED by both models  
**Impact:** Severe credibility issue — fundamental arithmetic error

### 2. Fabricated Entity: DigitalPersona Analytics Pty Ltd
**Articles:** 1, 3  
**Claim:** "DigitalPersona Analytics Pty Ltd sold census data at $2.50/record"  
**Verification:** No ASIC/ABN record. ABN lookup shows cancelled companies only.  
**Status:** ❌ DISPUTED by both models  

### 3. Non-existent Academic: Dr. Fiona McLean / ANU Center
**Articles:** 1, 3  
**Claim:** "Dr. Fiona McLean, Director of Center for Social Policy Research at ANU"  
**Verification:** ANU has no "Center for Social Policy Research." No faculty listing for Fiona McLean.  
**Status:** ❌ DISPUTED by both models  

### 4. Misattributed Research Center: RMIT Automotive Safety
**Article:** 6  
**Claim:** "RMIT Automotive Safety Research Centre"  
**Verification:** Centre for Automotive Safety Research is at **University of Adelaide**, not RMIT. Verified via adelaide.edu.au.  
**Status:** ❌ DISPUTED by both models  

### 5. Incorrect Population: White-bellied Sea Eagles
**Article:** 2  
**Claim:** "1,200 pairs currently"  
**Verification:** BirdLife International confirms 5,000-6,000 breeding pairs.  
**Status:** ❌ DISPUTED by both models  

### 6. Legal Impossibility: FOI on Meta Internal Emails
**Article:** 8  
**Claim:** "Internal Meta emails obtained via FOI"  
**Verification:** Australian FOI laws apply to government agencies only — cannot access US company records.  
**Status:** ❌ DISPUTED by both models  

### 7. Understated Compliance Rate: Social Media Ban
**Article:** 8  
**Claim:** "70% of under-16s retained access"  
**Verification:** BMJ study shows >86%; The Conversation shows >80%.  
**Status:** ❌ DISPUTED by both models  

### 8. Mathematical Error: Meta Penalty Percentage
**Article:** 8  
**Claim:** "$49.5M penalty < 0.01% of Meta's Australian revenue"  
**Math Check:** $49.5M ÷ $2.5B = ~2%, not 0.01%.  
**Status:** ❌ DISPUTED — Haiku flagged as math error, assistant flagged as calculation issue  

### 9. Geographic Error: Colombia Ring of Fire
**Article:** 5  
**Claim:** "Colombia's location along the Pacific Ring of Fire"  
**Verification:** Colombia is NOT on the Pacific Ring of Fire. The Ring encompasses Japan, Philippines, Indonesia, Peru, Chile, western North America, etc. Colombia sits above the Nazca-South American subduction zone but is not part of the Ring of Fire.  
**Status:** ❌ DISPUTED — Flagged only by Haiku (my model missed this)

### 10. Internal Contradiction: Scam Report Counts
**Articles:** 1, 3  
**Claim:** "12,000 reports within 6 hours" vs. "45,000 reports within 24 hours"  
**Issue:** 6-hour rate extrapolates to 48,000/24hr (12,000×4), which contradicts the stated 45,000/24hr (but not by 340% margin)  
**Status:** ⚠️ UNCERTAIN by both models — figures appear incompatible

### 11. Anecdotal Expert: Dr. Sarah Mitchell
**Articles:** 2, 6, 8  
**Claim:** "Dr. Sarah Mitchell" appears in three different contexts:
- Article 2: AIMS marine science (non-existent)
- Article 6: RMIT automotive safety (misattributed to RMIT, not Adelaide)  
- Article 8: University of Sydney digital rights  
**Status:** ⚠️ UNCERTAIN — Same name reused in different fields. Name is real but affiliations need verification.

### 12. Anecdotal Expert: Dr. Maria Santos

**Claim:** 
- Article 2: "Dr. Maria Santos, Australian Institute of Marine Science"

### 13. Taxonomic Misclassification

**Correction:** Orange-bellied parrot is a **seabird/parrot**, not a shorebird (shorebirds are wading birds like sandpipers)
