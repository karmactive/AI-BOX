Rather than attempting direct transcription, BUG FILMS preserved the spirit of Shirahama's aesthetic while working within animation production constraints. The result satisfied manga readers who appreciated fidelity without alienating viewers unfamiliar with the source.

This structure allows both anime newcomers and manga readers to progress through magical system discovery alongside the protagonist.
