# 007 First Light Gets Major "Behind Enemy Lines" Update with New Stealth Missions and 200+ Bug Fixes

IO Interactive's "Behind Enemy Lines" update for 007 First Light arrived on July 24, bringing new TacSim escalation missions and addressing over 200 player-reported issues discovered since launch.

The update adds two new escalation missions that challenge players through difficulty tiers. "The Workshop" forces players to eliminate targets using only silent elimination techniques without firearms—a constraint that reshapes how experienced players approach the level. "Extraction Avenues" focuses on escape mechanics, requiring players to reach extraction points before reinforcements arrive and complete lockdowns.

Three new weapons join the arsenal: the Stormberg 50 handgun, DRX-2 Machine Pistol, and DRS-7 Silenced SMG. Each fills specific playstyle niches across stealth, direct action, and mixed approaches.

What distinguishes this update is its responsiveness to community feedback. The 200-plus bug fixes target specific frustrations players reported since early access launch. Achievement-related bugs had prevented completion tracking for some players. Progression issues occasionally locked players out of campaign advancement. Crashes interrupted critical story moments. IO Interactive's specific focus on these reported problems demonstrates engagement with player experience during the critical launch window.

The Developer's Note in official patch documentation credits "community feedback and internal play testers" for identifying priority issues. This transparency about player-driven improvements matters for long-term community trust. Players see their reports acted upon within weeks rather than months, establishing that feedback drives development decisions.

Rewards follow predictable patterns. Unlocking the "Diamond in the Rough" outfit requires Intel currency earned through standard gameplay—avoiding premium currency entirely, which community feedback had specifically requested. This mirrors IO Interactive's approach in recent Hitman titles where cosmetic progression rewards engagement over payment.

The timing reflects standard post-launch cadence for major titles. Most games now deploy significant patches within 4-8 weeks addressing discovered issues and introducing fresh content to maintain player engagement. For 007 First Light, which launched through early access in late June before full launch in July, this update signaled continued development commitment.

All platforms received simultaneous updates: PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Xbox Series X, Xbox Series S, and PC. The patch size runs 8-10GB depending on platform, requiring 15-30 minutes on standard connections. IO Interactive's roadmap indicates more content planned for later in 2026, though specific details remain under wraps.

Players can download the update immediately. The expansion positions 007 First Light as an active live service receiving regular substantial content rather than a static launch experience.
